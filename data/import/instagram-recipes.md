## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup all purpose flour, spooned and leveled
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup low fat cottage cheese (2%)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil or melted butter
- 1/2 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- 2/3 cup blueberries, washed and dried
- salted butter, for cooking

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Blend all the wet ingredients (except the blueberries) until completely smooth.
3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir gently until a batter forms; fold in the blueberries and let the batter rest 5 minutes (no longer than 10).
4. Heat a nonstick pan to just below medium and coat with a thin layer of butter.
5. Cook 1/4-cup scoops for 3-4 minutes until the tops bubble and the bottoms are golden, then flip and cook through.
6. Repeat with the remaining batter, buttering the pan as needed; serve with maple syrup and berries.

## Notes

## Ingredients

- 2 servings of udon (frozen used)
- 225g sliced beef
- 1 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 4 stalks green onion, cut into 2-inch pieces (white and green parts separated)
- Sesame seeds, green onion, and nori seaweed, to garnish

### For the sauce

- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Loosen the frozen udon in hot water with chopsticks, then rinse under cold water and drain.

5. Return the noodles to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss to coat evenly.

## Ingredients

- 2 apples
- 1 lemon
- Turmeric + ginger (anti-inflammatory)
- 2 oranges
- Carrot

## Instructions

1. Wash and chop the apples, oranges, lemon, carrot, and ginger (plus turmeric, if using fresh) into pieces that fit the juicer chute.
2. Feed the pieces through the juicer, alternating fruits and vegetables to keep it running smoothly.
3. Collect the juice in a clean container and stir well.
4. Pour into small shot bottles with lids.
5. Refrigerate and drink one shot per serving; use within a few days.
