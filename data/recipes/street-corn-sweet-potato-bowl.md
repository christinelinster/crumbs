## Ingredients

- 4 sweet potatoes
- 1.5 lb 93/7 ground beef
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- salt/pepper to taste

### For the street corn

- 2 cups corn kernels
- 150g non fat plain greek yogurt
- 60g light mayo
- 60g cotija cheese
- 1/4 a red onion
- 1 large jalapeno
- juice of 2 limes
- cilantro
- 1 tsp chili powder
- salt to taste
- Tajin to taste

## Instructions

1. Bake the sweet potatoes at 425F for 45 minutes.
2. Cook the ground beef with the chili powder, garlic powder, cumin, salt, and pepper until done.
3. Combine all the street corn ingredients.
4. Divide everything into 4 meal-prep portions.
