## Ingredients

- 4-5 Persian cucumbers (or 3 Japanese cucumbers), cut into half moons, coins, or sticks
- 1 cubed avocado
- 1 finely diced large shallot (or 1/3 red onion)
- a large handful of finely chopped cilantro
- 2 tablespoons roasted pistachios
- 1 oz crumbled feta (plus more for topping)
- zest and juice of 1 large lime
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- generous pinch of salt
- 1/2 to 1 tbsp chile lime seasoning
- finely chopped cilantro, for topping

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumbers into half moons, coins, or sticks, keeping them thin enough to soak up the dressing.
2. Place the cucumbers in a bowl or jar.
3. Add the cubed avocado, diced shallot, and a large handful of chopped cilantro.
4. Sprinkle in the roasted pistachios.
5. Add the crumbled feta, lime zest and juice, olive oil, salt, and chile lime seasoning.
6. Seal the container and shake well, or toss everything gently in a bowl.
7. Finish with extra feta and cilantro on top; serve as-is or alongside a protein.

## Notes

Serve immediately; it pairs well with grilled chicken, shrimp, or steak.
