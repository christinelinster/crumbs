## Ingredients

- 3 hard boiled eggs
- 1 sourdough slice
- cherry tomatoes
- 2 bacon strips
- 2 tbsp 0% fat Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp of scallions
- salt & black pepper, to taste
- 1/2 small avocado
- olive oil (for roasting)

## Instructions

1. Toss the cherry tomatoes with olive oil and salt and roast in the air fryer or oven with the bacon for 15-20 minutes until extra crispy.
2. Boil the eggs if needed; mash them in a bowl until creamy.
3. Fold in the Greek yogurt, chopped scallions, chopped crispy bacon, salt, and black pepper; mix.
4. Toast the sourdough; spread the avocado evenly on top; add the egg salad and finish with the roasted cherry tomatoes.
