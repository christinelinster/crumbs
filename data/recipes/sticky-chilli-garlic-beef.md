## Ingredients

- 600g raw lean beef
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon onion powder
- 1 tablespoon chilli powder
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon garlic
- 1 teaspoon ginger
- chilli flakes
- 60ml light soy sauce
- sriracha (quantity not specified)
- 10g honey
- spring onion (quantity not specified)
- sesame seeds (quantity not specified)
- 200g uncooked washed rice

## Instructions

1. Season the beef with garlic powder, onion powder and chilli powder and toss to coat.
2. Heat sesame oil in a pan on high heat, add the garlic, ginger and chilli flakes, then the seasoned beef; cook 10-15 minutes until sizzling and browned.
3. Stir in the light soy sauce, sriracha, honey and spring onion and finish with sesame seeds.
4. While the beef cooks, boil the rice (200g uncooked yields about 630g cooked).
5. Divide the beef and rice equally into 5 meal prep containers.
