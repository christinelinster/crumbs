## Ingredients

- 400g fat-free high protein milk
- 40-45g 1:1 sugar substitute
- 1 Cometeer pod (can sub 1 shot espresso or 1 tbsp instant coffee)
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- A pinch of salt
- 1g (1/4 tsp) xanthan gum

### For the mix-ins

- 5 Oreo Thins (cream removed)

## Instructions

1. Blend all base ingredients until smooth, then freeze for at least 16 hours.
2. Run the frozen pint under hot water for at least 60 seconds.
3. Spin on 'Lite Ice Cream', then 'Respin' if the texture is powdery.
4. Make a hole down the centre with a butter knife, fill with the Oreo pieces, and run the 'Mix-in' cycle.

## Notes

Requires a Ninja Creami.
