## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast, diced into small pieces
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- garlic powder
- black pepper
- 50ml light soy sauce
- 200g uncooked washed white rice (makes roughly 630g cooked)

### For the honey garlic sauce

- 1 tsp coconut oil
- spring onions
- garlic paste
- 75ml soy sauce (add water if more sauce volume needed)
- hot sauce
- a drizzle of honey
- sesame seeds (optional)

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken and season with chilli powder, garlic powder, black pepper and soy sauce.
2. Cook in a pan 10-15 minutes on medium to high heat (or oven/air fry 20 minutes at 220C).
3. Boil the rice (200g uncooked makes roughly 630g cooked).
4. Warm the coconut oil in a pan on medium heat and add spring onions, garlic paste, soy sauce, hot sauce and a drizzle of honey.
5. Add the cooked chicken, simmer 5-10 minutes on low heat and stir well.
6. Portion into 5 containers with roughly 125g of cooked rice each and add sesame seeds if desired.
