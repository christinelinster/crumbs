## Ingredients

- 200g 0% fat Greek yogurt or protein yogurt
- 5 Medjool dates
- 40g 85%+ dark chocolate
- 1/2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- a pinch of flaky salt

## Instructions

1. Halve and pit the Medjool dates, then grill them for a few minutes until warm and caramelized.
2. Place the dates on parchment paper, cover with a second sheet and press flat with the bottom of a cup so they stick together into a base.
3. Spread the Greek yogurt over the date base and pour over the melted dark chocolate.
4. Drizzle with the olive oil and finish with flaky salt.
5. Freeze for at least 1 hour until firm, then cut into 9 squares and store in the freezer.
