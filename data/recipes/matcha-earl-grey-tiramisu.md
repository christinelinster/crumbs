## Ingredients

### For the earl grey mascarpone cream

- 3 egg yolks
- 80g granulated sugar
- 3 earl grey tea bags, cut open
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 230g (8oz) mascarpone
- 300g heavy cream, chilled

- A pack of ladyfingers (you won't use all)

### For the matcha soak

- 7g matcha powder, sifted
- 100g warm water
- 2 tsp sugar

## Instructions

1. Blitz the sugar and tea leaves to a fine powder, then whisk with the egg yolks over a double boiler until doubled in size; stir in the vanilla and salt.
2. Whip the mascarpone and heavy cream to medium peaks (do not over-whip), then gently fold in the egg yolk mixture.
3. Whisk the sifted matcha with warm water and sugar for the soak.
4. Dip ladyfingers in the matcha soak and line the base of the baking dish; optionally sift a layer of matcha over them.
5. Spread half the cream mixture over, then repeat the ladyfinger and cream layers.
6. Chill at least 5 hours (ideally overnight) and dust with matcha before serving.
