## Ingredients

- 1 block silken or soft tofu
- 2-3 eggs, whisked
- chopped scallion
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

### For the seasoning sauce

- 1/2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tbsp sesame oil
- a pinch of black pepper
- 2 tbsp water, adjust as needed

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs and combine all the seasoning sauce ingredients.
2. Heat the oil in a pan and add the tofu and whisked eggs.
3. Pour the seasoning sauce over and let it simmer until the eggs are set and the flavours meld.
4. Top with chopped scallion and serve over rice.
