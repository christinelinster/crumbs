## Ingredients

- 1 chocolate protein shake (350-400ml)
- 20-25g monk fruit sweetener
- 1 tsp red velvet extract (bakery emulsion)
- A pinch of salt
- 1g (1/4 tsp) xanthan gum

### For the topping

- 1/2 red velvet brownie (chopped)

## Instructions

1. Blend all base ingredients until smooth, then freeze for at least 16 hours.
2. Run the frozen pint under hot water for at least 60 seconds.
3. Spin on 'Lite Ice Cream', respinning if the texture is powdery.
4. Top with the chopped brownie pieces and mix in by hand.

## Notes

Requires a Ninja Creami.
