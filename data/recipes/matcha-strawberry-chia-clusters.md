## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups finely diced strawberries, fresh (frozen works too)
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 cup white chocolate (thinned with 1 tsp coconut oil or melted in a double boiler)
- 2 tsp matcha powder

## Instructions

1. Finely dice the strawberries and stir them together with the Greek yogurt and chia seeds in a bowl.
2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and scoop the mixture into round clusters, about 8 portions.
3. Freeze the clusters until completely firm, at least 2 hours.
4. Melt the white chocolate with the coconut oil (microwave in short bursts or use a double boiler) and whisk in the matcha powder until smooth and evenly green.
5. Dip each frozen cluster into the matcha white chocolate, letting the excess drip off.
6. Place back on the lined sheet and chill until the coating is set, then serve or store in the freezer.
