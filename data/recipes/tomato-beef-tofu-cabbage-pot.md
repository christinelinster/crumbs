## Ingredients

- 150g shabu shabu beef
- 150g tofu, cubed
- 1 cup cabbage
- 1 medium tomato
- 1 pack vermicelli noodles (optional)
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp white vinegar
- 2 cups water
- 1 tbsp oil (from steps)

## Instructions

1. Heat a pot over medium-high heat with 1 tbsp oil and saute the minced garlic.
2. Add the tomatoes and salt; stir-fry for 2 minutes.
3. Layer on the cabbage slices, tofu, and sliced beef; pour over the soy sauce, vinegar, and water; cook 5 minutes and enjoy.
