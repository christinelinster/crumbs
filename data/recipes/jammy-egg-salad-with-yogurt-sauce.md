## Ingredients

- 4 soft-boiled eggs

### For the yogurt sauce

- 2 big tbsp of Greek yogurt
- 1 clove of minced garlic
- 1 tsp of salt and pepper
- A bit of lemon juice (about 1tbsp)
- Minced red onion
- Grated cucumber (squeeze the water out)

## Instructions

1. Cover the eggs with water in a pot and bring to a boil.
2. Add the eggs and cook for 6 minutes.
3. Transfer to ice water for 5-10 minutes, then peel.
4. Stir together the yogurt, garlic, salt, pepper, lemon juice, onion, and cucumber to make the sauce.
5. Add the eggs to the sauce and mix.
6. Cut the eggs open just before eating and serve.
