## Ingredients

- 160g glutinous rice flour
- 60g white sugar
- 200ml whole milk (room temp)
- 30g unsalted butter (~2.12 tbsp, room temp)
- 1 egg (room temp)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Bring the milk, butter, and egg to room temperature. Melt the butter and milk together in a pot.
2. In a bowl, mix the egg, sugar, salt, and vanilla extract.
3. Add the warm butter and milk to the egg mixture, then mix in the glutinous rice flour.
4. Strain the batter for a smooth texture (pro tip).
5. Pour into a baking dish and bake at 365°F for 30 minutes.
