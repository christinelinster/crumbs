## Ingredients

- oil
- shallots
- 1 lb ground chicken
- 2 spoonfuls oyster sauce
- 1 splash soy sauce
- 1 splash fish sauce
- 1 cup holy basil or Thai basil
- 2 peppers (Serrano or Thai)
- silken tofu
- 1 egg (for a sunny-side-up egg)

## Instructions

1. Heat oil in a pan and crisp the shallots.
2. Add the ground chicken and brown it, stirring in the oyster sauce, soy sauce, and fish sauce.
3. When the chicken is about half cooked, add the basil and peppers and cook through.
4. Serve with silken tofu and a sunny-side-up egg.
