## Ingredients

- 28oz chicken breast, cubed
- 650g cooked rice (280g dry weight)
- 1/2 cup chopped green onion
- 1/2 cup onions

### For the marinade

- 1 tbsp chili crisp
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tbsp pepper
- 1/2 tbsp gochugaru
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp avocado oil

### For the honey garlic sauce

- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp chili crisp
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 4 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 tbsp gochugaru

## Instructions

1. Toss the cubed chicken in the marinade ingredients.
2. Cook the chicken through in a pan or skillet.
3. Stir in the honey garlic sauce mixture and let it simmer briefly to coat.
4. Serve over the cooked rice, topped with the green onion and onion.

## Notes

Gochugaru can be swapped for red chili flakes.
