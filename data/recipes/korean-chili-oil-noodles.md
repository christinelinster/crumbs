## Ingredients

- 120g knife cut noodles
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp reserved noodle cooking water
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp gochujang or Korean chili paste
- 1.5 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1/2 tsp chicken powder
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- sesame seeds and scallions/spring onions for garnish

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles according to package instructions and transfer to a bowl.
2. Add the sauce ingredients and noodles together and mix well, loosening with reserved noodle water as needed.
3. Garnish with sesame seeds and spring onions and serve.

## Notes

Adjust the amount of oil to taste.
