## Ingredients

- 15g isolate protein
- 20g whey
- 30g Greek yogurt
- 150g sugar-free almond milk
- 50g milk (2% fat)
- a pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
- sweetener, if not sweet enough

### For the topping

- 50g apple, cooked with cinnamon and water
- 7g sugar-free biscuit, crumbled

## Instructions

1. Blend the isolate, whey, Greek yogurt, almond milk, regular milk, salt, xanthan gum and sweetener until smooth.
2. Freeze in the Ninja Creami pint until fully set.
3. Spin in the Ninja Creami and re-spin if needed until creamy.
4. Top with the cinnamon-cooked apple and crumbled biscuit before serving.
