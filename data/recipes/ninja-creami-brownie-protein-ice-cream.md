## Ingredients

- 330ml chocolate protein shake
- 6g dark cacao powder
- a pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
- sweetener, if not sweet enough

### For the topping

- 40g homemade protein brownies
- 3g chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Blend the protein shake, cacao powder, salt, xanthan gum and sweetener until smooth.
2. Freeze in the Ninja Creami pint until fully set.
3. Spin in the Ninja Creami and re-spin if needed until creamy.
4. Top with the protein brownies and chocolate chips before serving.
