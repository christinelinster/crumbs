## Ingredients

- 1 lb beef (or any protein), bite-size pieces
- 1 large carrot, chopped
- 1 large potato, chopped
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fruit jam or honey
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce or soy sauce
- 2 cups bone broth or low sodium stock
- 2-4 oz Japanese curry roux

## Instructions

1. Cut the protein and vegetables into bite-size pieces.
2. Combine everything in a pot and bring to a boil, then lower to a medium-low simmer.
3. Cook 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the meat and vegetables are tender and the sauce is as thick as you like.
4. Stir well to fully dissolve the roux, then serve warm over rice.

## Notes

Serve with rice.
