## Ingredients

- 1 medium ripe banana (~100g)
- 1 scoop (40g) vegan salted caramel protein powder
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- Dash of cinnamon
- 1 tbsp (15ml) maple syrup
- 1/4 cup (60ml) almond milk (can use any milk or just water)
- 2 tbsp chocolate chips + extra for on top (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mash the banana to a puree in a bowl.
2. Mix in the protein powder, baking powder, cinnamon, maple syrup and milk until the powder is fully dissolved.
3. Stir in the chocolate chips.
4. Transfer to a microwave-safe ramekin, top with extra chips if desired, and microwave on high for 90 seconds (watching for overflow).
