## Ingredients

- 2 large sweet potatoes
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 spring onion, chopped
- 1/2 red pepper, finely chopped
- handful of feta cheese
- salt, pepper and oil (for baking)

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 200C; wash, dry and prick the sweet potatoes, brush with oil and bake 45-50 minutes until soft (or air fry 25 minutes / microwave).
2. Let the potatoes cool slightly, then beat the eggs with the milk.
3. Halve the sweet potatoes, gently mash the flesh and form a well in each half.
4. Season with salt and pepper, add the spring onion and red pepper, pour in the egg mixture and top with feta.
5. Bake or air fry 15-20 more minutes until the egg is set.
