## Ingredients

- 30g whey
- 100g protein milk (fairlife-type)
- 100g milk (2% fat)
- a pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
- sweetener, if not sweet enough

### For the topping

- 25g strawberries
- 12g sugar-free biscuit, crumbled

## Instructions

1. Blend the whey, protein milk, regular milk, salt, xanthan gum and sweetener until smooth.
2. Freeze in the Ninja Creami pint until fully set.
3. Spin in the Ninja Creami and re-spin if needed until creamy.
4. Top with strawberries and crumbled biscuit before serving.
