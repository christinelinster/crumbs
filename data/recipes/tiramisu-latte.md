## Ingredients

### For the shaken espresso

- 2 shots of espresso
- a pinch of flaky sea salt
- a drizzle of vanilla bean paste
- a little cocoa powder

### For the mascarpone cold foam

- milk
- mascarpone
- a drizzle of vanilla bean paste

### For assembly

- chocolate sauce
- ice
- cocoa powder, for dusting

## Instructions

1. Combine the espresso, salt, vanilla bean paste and cocoa powder, then shake until light and foamy.
2. Froth the milk, mascarpone and vanilla bean paste until thick and foamy.
3. Coat the inside of a glass with chocolate sauce and add ice.
4. Pour in the shaken espresso, top with the mascarpone cold foam and dust with cocoa powder.
