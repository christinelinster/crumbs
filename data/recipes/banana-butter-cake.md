## Ingredients

- 120g unsalted butter, softened
- 150-175g granulated white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 5ml vanilla essence
- 3 bananas, mashed (approx 200g)
- 100ml milk
- 190g flour (sift if clumpy)
- 3g baking powder

## Instructions

1. Cream the softened butter for a couple of minutes, then beat with the sugar until light and fluffy (about 2 minutes).
2. Whisk in the eggs, then the vanilla.
3. Stir in the mashed banana and milk (lumps from cold ingredients are fine).
4. Sift in the flour and baking powder, folding until no flour streaks remain.
5. Pour into an 8-inch baking tray, tap to release air bubbles, and bake at 160°C for 45-50 minutes until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
