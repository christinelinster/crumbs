## Ingredients

- 375 g medium tofu
- 5g sesame oil
- 1/2 white onion
- 1 zucchini
- 100g mushrooms
- 80 g kimchi
- 1 egg
- 3 spring onions
- 3 cups chicken or dashi stock

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Splenda / stevia packets
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 40 g gochujang
- 1 tbsp gochugaru
- 3 cloves garlic minced

## Instructions

1. Prep everything: slice the onion, zucchini, mushrooms, tofu, and spring onions; mince the garlic.
2. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, mirin, stevia, and garlic in a small bowl until smooth and thick.
3. Layer onion and spring onions at the base of a pot; arrange the mushrooms, zucchini, and tofu around the center; add the kimchi and the sauce mix on top.
4. Pour in the stock; bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer so the flavors blend.
5. Make a well in the center, crack in the egg, and cook until soft or jammy.
6. Top with chopped spring onions and serve bubbling hot, best with rice or noodles on the side.

## Notes

Serve with rice or noodles.
