## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup hot water
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 1/2 tablespoons oyster sauce (or vegetarian oyster sauce)
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine (or dry cooking sherry)
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon dark soy sauce
- 1/8 teaspoon white pepper
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic (about 6 cloves garlic; divided)
- 1 pound Chinese or Japanese eggplants (2-3 eggplants)
- 4 1/2 tablespoons neutral oil (such as vegetable oil, canola oil, avocado oil, or peanut oil)
- 4 ounces ground pork (or ground chicken, optional)
- 2 teaspoons minced ginger
- 5-10 dried red chilies (de-seeded and sliced into small pieces)
- 2 scallions (chopped)


## Instructions

1. Stir the sugar into the hot water until dissolved. Mix in the cornstarch, oyster sauce, rice vinegar, Shaoxing wine, light and dark soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper, and half the garlic. Set aside.
2. Trim the eggplants and cut them into bite-size chunks on an angle, turning them as you cut. Cut them just before cooking to prevent browning.
3. Heat a wok over medium-high heat until lightly smoking. Add 1½ tablespoons oil and cook the eggplant in one layer over medium heat for 3-4 minutes, turning until lightly browned.
4. Add another 1½ tablespoons oil. Cook for 4 minutes, until soft and slightly translucent. Work in batches if needed, then transfer to a plate.
5. Raise the heat to high and add the remaining 1½ tablespoons oil. Add the pork, if using, and cook until opaque, breaking it up. Add the ginger, chilies, and remaining garlic. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
6. Return the eggplant and toss. Stir the sauce, add it to the wok, and simmer until it coats a spoon. Stir in the scallions and serve.
