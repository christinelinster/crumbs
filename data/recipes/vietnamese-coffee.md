## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons Vietnamese ground coffee (we used Trung Nguyen brand)
- 1-3 tablespoons sweetened condensed milk
- 6-8 ounces water that is close to boiling point (depending on your desired coffee strength)


## Instructions

1. Use Trung Nguyen ground coffee or another French roast.
2. Use a 6-ounce Phin filter, French press, or drip coffee maker.
3. Measure 3 tablespoons ground coffee and spread it evenly in the filter.
4. Set the metal filter gently on top. Do not shake or compress the grounds; they can clog the holes.
5. Pour condensed milk to taste into a mug or heatproof glass.
6. Measure 6 ounces near-boiling water, or 8 ounces for a milder coffee.
7. Set the filter over the mug. Pour in 2 tablespoons hot water and let the grounds bloom for 5 seconds.
8. Gently press the filter to compact the bloomed grounds.
9. Slowly pour the remaining water into the filter.
10. Let the coffee drip for about 5 minutes.
11. Remove the filter and stir the coffee with the condensed milk.
