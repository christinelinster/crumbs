## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs
- 1/2 cup diced yellow onion
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 1 1/2 cups finely chopped broccoli (about 1 small head)
- 3/4 cup almond flour
- 1/4 cup coconut flour
- 1/2 cup collagen peptides (optional)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup cottage cheese or farmer's cheese
- 1/2 cup shredded mild cheddar
- salt and pepper (to taste)
- 1/2 tablespoon avocado oil (for cooking the vegetables)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the 1/2 tablespoon avocado oil, onion, garlic, and broccoli. Cook until the onion and broccoli have softened, stirring occasionally.
3. Whisk the eggs until smooth and season with salt and pepper.
4. Add the vegetable mixture, almond flour, coconut flour, baking powder, salt, black pepper, collagen, and cottage cheese. Stir until a thick batter forms, then let it sit for about 5 minutes.
5. Scoop about 2-3 tablespoons of batter onto the baking sheet per biscuit. Top with a sprinkle of shredded cheese.
6. Bake for 18-25 minutes, until golden and firm to the touch.

## Notes

Store in the refrigerator for 4-5 days.
