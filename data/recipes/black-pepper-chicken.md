## Ingredients

- 500g chicken thigh, diced
- sesame seeds (garnish)

### For the marinade

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1/2 tbsp neutral oil
- a pinch of salt and pepper

### For the sauce

- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- a pinch of salt

### For the stir fry

- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- 2 tsp ginger, minced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 brown onion, chopped
- 2 bell peppers, chopped (mixed colors used)

## Instructions

1. Whisk together all the marinade ingredients (light soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch, oil, salt and pepper) and toss with the diced chicken thigh; marinate briefly.
2. Whisk the sauce ingredients (chicken broth, soy sauces, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch, sugar, black pepper, salt) in a measuring cup until smooth.
3. Heat the neutral oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat and sear the marinated chicken until just cooked through; remove to a plate.
4. In the same pan, stir-fry the minced ginger, garlic, and chopped onion until fragrant, then add the chopped bell peppers and cook until just softened.
5. Return the chicken to the pan, pour in the sauce, and stir until it bubbles and thickens into a glossy coating.
6. Plate over rice and garnish with sesame seeds.
