## Ingredients

- 1 cup chopped strawberries
- 1 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- dark chocolate for melting

## Instructions

1. Stir the chopped strawberries, Greek yogurt, and maple syrup together.
2. Shape the mixture into clusters on a parchment-lined sheet.
3. Freeze for 2-3 hours.
4. Drizzle with melted dark chocolate and enjoy.

## Notes
