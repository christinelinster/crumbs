## Ingredients

- 1/4 medium onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 225g chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 small tomato, diced
- 1 cup chicken broth (sub water)
- 1 cup water
- 1 serving udon
- salt to taste

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 2 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Warm 1 tbsp oil in a small pot over medium heat and soften the sliced onion and minced garlic until translucent, about 3 minutes.
2. Stir in the diced tomato and cook a further 3 minutes.
3. Add the chicken pieces and all sauce ingredients; stir-fry about 2 minutes, then pour in the water and simmer roughly 5 minutes.
4. Add the udon and stir through for about a minute, then garnish with chopped green onion and serve.
