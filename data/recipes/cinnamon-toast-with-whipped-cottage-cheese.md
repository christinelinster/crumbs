## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp granulated sugar (cinnamon sugar)
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon (cinnamon sugar)
- a pinch of flaky sea salt (cinnamon sugar)
- 1/2 cup 2% or 4% small-curd cottage cheese
- 1/4 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 2 slices bread
- salted or unsalted butter (optional)
- fresh fruit, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. Mix the sugar, cinnamon and salt to make the cinnamon sugar.
2. Whip the cottage cheese in a mini food processor until creamy, then stir in the vanilla and half the cinnamon sugar.
3. Toast the bread and spread with butter if desired, then spread the whipped cottage cheese mixture edge to edge.
4. Sprinkle with the remaining cinnamon sugar.
5. Air fry at 400F until bubbling and starting to brown, about 5 minutes (or use a toaster oven or broiler).
6. Serve with fresh fruit if desired.
