## Ingredients

- 3oz turkey sausage, raw
- 2 eggs, scrambled
- 2 eggs, whisked
- 3 sheets rice paper
- 1/4 cup fat free mozzarella cheese
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs in a bowl.
2. Brown the sausage in a pan over medium heat, then scramble the eggs in the same pan on low.
3. Dip each rice paper sheet in the whisked egg and lay the three sheets overlapping on oiled parchment.
4. Arrange the sausage, cheese, and scrambled eggs down the middle, then roll into a long burrito.
5. Curl the burrito into a spiral, sprinkle with sesame seeds, and mist with oil.
6. Bake at 400F for 15 minutes until crisp and golden.
