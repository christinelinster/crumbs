## Ingredients

- 150g skyr
- 100g protein milk
- 15g whey
- a pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
- sweetener, if not sweet enough
- 37g protein cookie dough (topping; recipe is on the creator's page)

## Instructions

1. Blend the skyr, protein milk, whey, salt, xanthan gum and sweetener until smooth.
2. Freeze in the Ninja Creami pint until fully set.
3. Spin in the Ninja Creami and re-spin if needed until creamy.
4. Top with the protein cookie dough before serving.
