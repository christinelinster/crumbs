## Ingredients

- Baked potato (large, 6-8 oz Yukon gold)
- Cottage cheese
- Shredded cheddar
- Chives
- Black pepper
- Salt

## Instructions

1. Bake a large Yukon gold potato directly on the oven rack at 325F for 90 minutes, or use any baked potato.
2. Split it open and stuff with cottage cheese, shredded cheddar, chives, black pepper, and salt to taste.
3. Return to a 375F oven for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and browned in spots; broil for a crispier top.
