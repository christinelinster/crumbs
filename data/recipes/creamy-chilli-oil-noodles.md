## Ingredients

- 100g low-fat high-protein paneer or cottage cheese (or 120g silken tofu)
- 1 tbsp unsweetened peanut butter (16g)
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey (7g)
- pinch of salt
- 4-6 tbsp hot water, as needed to blend
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tsp grated garlic
- 1 tbsp chopped spring onion whites
- 1 tsp chili flakes or chili crisp without the oil
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- black pepper, to taste
- 1 tbsp neutral oil, hot
- 200g shirataki noodles, washed & drained
- spring onion greens, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Blend the paneer, peanut butter, soy sauce, honey, salt, and hot water into a smooth creamy sauce.
2. Combine the ginger, garlic, spring onion whites, chili flakes, sesame seeds, soy sauce, sugar, pepper, and salt in a heatproof bowl, then pour over the hot oil and stir.
3. Mix the chili oil through the blended paneer sauce.
4. Toss with the shirataki noodles, garnish with spring onion greens, and serve right away.
