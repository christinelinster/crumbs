## Ingredients

- 200g cream cheese
- 60g sugar
- 2 eggs
- 20g flour (thin/cake flour)
- 100cc fresh cream
- 2 tbsp matcha milk (sweetened matcha milk powder)

## Instructions

1. Warm the cream in the microwave at 600W for 30-40 seconds and stir in the matcha milk until combined.
2. Place the cream cheese in the rice cooker bowl and soften it with a rubber spatula.
3. Mix in the sugar until smooth.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each.
5. Stir in the matcha-cream mixture and combine thoroughly.
6. Run the rice cooker on a normal cycle (cake mode, ~45 minutes if available).
7. Let cool, remove from the bowl and chill at least 2 hours before serving.
