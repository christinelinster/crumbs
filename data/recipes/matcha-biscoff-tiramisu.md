## Ingredients

- 3 large egg yolks
- 70g granulated sugar
- 225g mascarpone cheese (room temperature)
- 350g whipping cream
- 2 tsp matcha powder (sifted)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp matcha powder
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar
- 120g hot water
- 25-30 Biscoff cookies

### For the topping

- 2 tsp matcha powder
- 2 Biscoff cookies for decoration

## Instructions

1. Whisk the egg yolks and sugar over a double boiler for about 5 minutes until pale, then blend in the mascarpone until smooth.
2. Whip the cream with the matcha and vanilla to medium-stiff peaks, then fold into the mascarpone mixture.
3. Whisk the matcha, sugar and hot water until slightly frothy for the soak.
4. Dip the Biscoff cookies briefly in the matcha soak and layer them over the base of the serving dish.
5. Spread over a layer of matcha cream, then repeat the layers, finishing with cream.
6. Chill at least 5 hours (preferably overnight), then dust with matcha and decorate with cookies before serving.
