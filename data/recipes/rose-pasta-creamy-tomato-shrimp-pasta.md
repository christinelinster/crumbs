## Ingredients

- pasta
- 7-10 tomatoes
- shrimp
- 1 onion
- spinach
- minced garlic
- 4 tbsp tomato sauce
- cream cheese
- 1.5 tbsp chicken stock
- 400ml water
- 200ml milk
- butter
- cheese

## Instructions

1. Add the pasta, tomatoes, shrimp, onion, spinach, garlic, tomato sauce, cream cheese, chicken stock, water, and milk to one pot and bring to a boil.
2. Cook everything together, removing the shrimp once cooked if you want to avoid overcooking them.
3. When the pasta is almost done, add the butter and cheese and simmer until the sauce thickens.
