## Ingredients

- 1/2 tbsp salty vanilla syrup
- double shot of espresso
- ice
- 3-4oz milk

### For the honey cold foam

- 2 tbsp heavy cream
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp honey

## Instructions

1. Stir the salty vanilla syrup into the espresso shots.
2. Add ice and the milk.
3. Whip the heavy cream, milk and honey until foamy and pour over the top.
