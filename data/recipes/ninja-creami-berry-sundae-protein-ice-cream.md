## Ingredients

- 200g sugar-free almond milk
- 100g protein milk
- 25g whey
- 30g banana
- 7g protein pudding mix
- a pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp xanthan gum
- sweetener, if not sweet enough

### For the topping

- 30g sugar-free jam, thinned with water

## Instructions

1. Blend the almond milk, protein milk, whey, banana, pudding mix, salt, xanthan gum and sweetener until smooth.
2. Freeze in the Ninja Creami pint until fully set.
3. Spin in the Ninja Creami and re-spin if needed until creamy.
4. Top with the thinned jam before serving.
