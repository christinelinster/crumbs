## Ingredients

- 210 grams all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 90 grams browned butter, re-softened not melted (roughly 1.5 sticks)
- 80 grams dark brown sugar (1/2 cup)
- 50 grams granulated sugar (1/4 cup)
- 1 egg, room temperature
- 1 ripened banana
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup chopped toffee

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together in a bowl and set aside.
2. Beat the re-softened browned butter with the dark brown sugar and granulated sugar until creamy and fluffy.
3. Mash the ripe banana, then beat the egg, mashed banana, and vanilla extract into the butter mixture until smooth.
4. Add the dry ingredients in two or three additions, mixing just until combined.
5. Fold in the chopped toffee.
6. Chill the dough in the refrigerator until firm (the video rests it 48 hours to develop flavor and gloss).
7. Scoop into balls, place on a lined baking sheet, and bake at 325°F (165°C) for 15-20 minutes, until the edges are set and golden. Cool on the tray a few minutes before moving to a rack.
