## Ingredients

- 200g frozen sliced beef, thawed (cut into ~8cm pieces if needed)
- 1/2 large brown onion, thinly sliced
- 4 eggs (gently break yolks with chopsticks, lightly loosen whites - do not beat)
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced
- 2 servings steamed rice
- 150ml dashi stock (or 150ml water + 2 tsp dashi powder)
- 2.25 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp sake
- 2-3 tbsp premium soy sauce (to taste)
- 2-3 tsp sugar (to taste)
- Japanese chilli powder, optional

## Instructions

1. Stir the dashi, mirin, sake, soy sauce, and sugar in a jug until the sugar dissolves.
2. Saute the onion in oil over medium heat for about half a minute.
3. Add half the sauce and spread the onions evenly.
4. Lay half the beef in a single layer over the onions.
5. Raise the heat to medium-high, cover, and cook about 10 seconds until the beef is half pink.
6. Lower the heat slightly, drizzle over half the eggs without stirring, cover again, and cook until the eggs are about 70% set - soft, not overcooked.
7. Slide over one serving of rice, garnish with spring onion and chilli powder.
8. Repeat with the remaining ingredients for the second bowl.
