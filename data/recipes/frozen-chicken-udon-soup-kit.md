## Ingredients

- 140g japanese udon noodles
- 8oz raw chicken thigh
- 2 cups snow white mushroom
- 2 cups napa cabbage
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp onion powder
- 2 green onions, medium
- 1 cup chicken bone broth
- 1 cup water
- salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Chop the cabbage and chicken into 1-inch pieces and thinly slice the green onions.
2. Mix the garlic, miso, cabbage, soy sauce, sesame oil, black pepper, and onion powder into a sauce.
3. Divide the mushrooms, chicken, udon, cabbage sauce, and green onions between two meal-prep containers and freeze overnight.
4. When ready to eat, loosen the frozen kit in the microwave for 15 seconds, then boil with the bone broth and water until cooked through.
5. Season with salt and enjoy.
