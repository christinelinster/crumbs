## Ingredients

- 300g boneless chicken thigh
- 4 eggs
- 1/2 cup dashi stock
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp mirin (or rice wine)
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 onion
- Neutral cooking oil
- Green onion

## Instructions

1. Mix the dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar into a sauce.
2. Slice the onion into thin strips.
3. Whisk the eggs, leaving some yolk intact for color.
4. Sear the chicken in an oiled hot pan, skin side down first, until lightly browned; cut into bite-sized pieces.
5. Stir-fry the onion until just softened.
6. Pour in the sauce and simmer 4-5 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
7. Drizzle in the eggs and let them barely set, then top with green onion and serve over rice.

## Notes

Serve over rice.
