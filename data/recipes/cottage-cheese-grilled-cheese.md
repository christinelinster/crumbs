## Ingredients

- 1 cup cottage cheese (blend smooth)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- Salt + pepper
- 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella
- 1/4 cup shredded cheddar
- 2 tbsp grated parmesan

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, and seasonings until smooth.
3. Fold in the mozzarella, cheddar, and parmesan.
4. Spread the mixture into two thin rectangles on the sheet.
5. Bake 18-22 minutes until set and golden.
6. Cool briefly, flip one piece, add extra cheese on top, and cover with the second piece.
7. Sear in a pan like a grilled cheese until crisp on both sides.
