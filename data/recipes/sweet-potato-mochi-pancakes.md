## Ingredients

- 300g sweet potatoes (roughly 2 small sweet potatoes)
- 65g sugar (adjust to taste)
- 50ml milk
- 130g glutinous rice flour

## Instructions

1. Peel and slice the sweet potatoes, then steam until completely tender.
2. While still warm, mash the sweet potatoes with the sugar until smooth.
3. Stir in the milk, then add the glutinous rice flour and knead into a smooth, pliable dough.
4. Divide the dough and shape into small, flat pancakes.
5. Heat a lightly oiled pan or griddle over low heat and cook the pancakes low and slow, flipping once, until golden brown on both sides and cooked through.
6. Serve warm (the creator garnishes with sesame seeds, optional).
