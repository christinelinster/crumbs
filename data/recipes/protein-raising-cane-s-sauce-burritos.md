## Ingredients

- 3 lbs ground beef
- salt, pepper, garlic powder (to season beef)
- 10 eggs
- 2 tomatoes
- one bag fat-free cheddar cheese
- 200g cooked rice
- prepared sauce
- large tortillas (Mission Carb Balance)

### For the Cane's-style sauce

- 400g plain Greek yogurt
- 150g light sour cream
- 20g mustard
- 20g sugar-free ketchup
- 20g lemon juice
- 10g Worcestershire sauce
- 6g paprika powder
- 6g garlic powder
- 4g onion powder
- 2g salt
- 5g honey

## Instructions

1. Stir together all the protein Raising Cane's sauce ingredients.
2. Cook the ground beef with salt, pepper, and garlic powder; crack the eggs into the side of the pan and scramble them alongside.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes, cheddar, rice, and the prepared sauce; mix everything together to finish the filling.
4. Place two scoops of filling onto a large tortilla and roll it up tightly like the video.
5. Toast the burrito in a pan seam-side down first, then on both sides until golden.
6. To reheat from frozen: air fry directly from frozen at 350F (175C) for 12-15 minutes, flipping halfway.
