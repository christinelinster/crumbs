## Ingredients

- 1.5 go (rice-cooker cups) white rice
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 250g oil-free canned tuna
- 1/2 tbsp chicken bouillon granules
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Add the rice, sesame oil, tuna, chicken bouillon, soy sauce, salt, and pepper to the rice cooker with the usual amount of water.
2. Cook on the normal rice setting, then fluff and mix before serving.
