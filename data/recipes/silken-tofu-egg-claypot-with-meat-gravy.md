## Ingredients

- cooking spray, 1-second spray
- 1 pack silken tofu (9 oz)
- 2 large eggs

### For the meat gravy

- cooking spray, 1-second spray
- 1/2 stalk scallions
- 2 cloves garlic
- 4 oz extra lean ground chicken
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy
- 2 tsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp monkfruit sugar
- dash white pepper powder
- 1 tsp corn starch
- 2 tbsp water (for slurry)

## Instructions

1. Brown the garlic, scallions and ground chicken in a sprayed pan over medium heat.
2. Stir in the sauces and the corn starch slurry, then simmer for 2 minutes; set aside any extra gravy.
3. Coat a claypot with cooking spray, add the tofu and break it up with a spoon.
4. Pour in the beaten eggs, then layer the meat gravy over the top.
5. Cover and cook on medium for 3 minutes until the eggs are set; mix and serve.
