## Ingredients

- 32oz 93% ground beef
- 8oz pork chorizo
- 1 tbsp coarse salt
- 1 tbsp ground paprika
- 1 tbsp ground oregano
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1.5 tsp ground black pepper
- 120g red enchilada sauce
- 14oz (400g) can fire roasted tomatoes
- 2 tbsp garlic puree
- 1 tbsp chipotle peppers
- 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- 2 orange bell peppers
- 2 red bell peppers
- 1 onion
- 672g pasta, cooked to 50% (half the box time)
- 800g 2% cottage cheese
- 100g 1/3 fat cream cheese
- 150g extra sharp cheddar cheese
- 30g cheddar powder
- 240g red enchilada sauce
- 300ml milk
- salt and pepper to taste
- chopped cilantro (optional)

## Instructions

1. Bake the peppers and onion at 400F for 20-25 minutes.
2. Slow cook the meats with the spice blend, enchilada sauce, tomatoes, garlic, chipotle, adobo, and vinegar on High 2-3 hours or Low 3-4 hours.
3. Stir in the half-cooked pasta and the blended queso sauce.
4. Cover and cook on High 15-20 minutes.
5. Divide into 10 containers, freeze, and reheat with 2-3 tbsp milk per portion for a creamier sauce.
