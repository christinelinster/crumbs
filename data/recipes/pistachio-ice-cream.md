## Ingredients

- 70g pistachios
- 2 medjool dates, pitted
- 350ml semi-skimmed milk
- 25g vanilla protein powder
- Pinch of sea salt

### For the mix-ins

- 10g crushed pistachios
- 20g white chocolate chunks

## Instructions

1. Blend all base ingredients together and pour into the Ninja Creami tub.
2. Freeze overnight.
3. Process on the ice cream function until creamy.
4. Add the pistachios and white chocolate and use the 'mix-in' function to distribute them.
5. Optionally top with more crushed pistachios before serving.

## Notes

Requires a Ninja Creami.
