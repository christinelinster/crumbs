## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup shredded carrot
- 1/2 cup shredded cabbage
- 2 eggs
- 1/8 cup flour
- Salt/pepper
- Optional dipping: soy sauce & chili crisp

## Instructions

1. Add the shredded carrot, shredded cabbage, eggs, flour, and salt and pepper to a bowl and whisk into a loose batter.
2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and spray lightly with olive oil.
3. Scoop small portions of the mixture into the pan and press each down into a flat cake.
4. Cook until golden brown on the bottom, then flip and cook until the second side is golden and set through.
5. Serve warm with soy sauce and chili crisp for dipping.
