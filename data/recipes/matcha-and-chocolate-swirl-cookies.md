## Ingredients

- 110g butter, melted or browned
- 105g sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 160g flour (80g per dough)
- 1/2 tbsp matcha
- 1/2 tsp baking soda (1/4 tsp per dough)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder (1/4 tsp per dough)
- pinch of salt (per dough)
- 63g white chocolate chips
- 63g dark chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Whisk the melted butter, sugar, egg, and vanilla together.
2. Split the mixture evenly between two bowls.
3. For the matcha dough, combine 80g flour, the matcha, 1/4 tsp baking soda, 1/4 tsp baking powder, and a pinch of salt; add to the first bowl along with the white chocolate chips.
4. For the chocolate dough, combine 80g flour, 1/4 tsp baking soda, 1/4 tsp baking powder, and a pinch of salt; add to the second bowl along with the dark chocolate chips.
5. Mix each dough until no flour streaks remain.
6. Pinch the two doughs together randomly into 6 balls of about 90g each.
7. Bake 10-15 minutes at 170C; or freeze the balls first, then bake 15-20 minutes at 160C.
