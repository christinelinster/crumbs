## Ingredients

- 1 serving udon noodles
- 1-2 chicken thighs or drumsticks, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup mushrooms (shiitake or button), diced
- 1/4 onion, sliced
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp oyster sauce (optional)
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1 tbsp oil (for cooking)
- 2 cups water

## Instructions

1. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp shaoxing wine, 1 tsp salt, and 1 tsp pepper; marinate 15 minutes.
2. Sear the chicken skin-side down in hot oil until browned and cooked through, 5-7 minutes.
3. Add the onion and saute 2-3 minutes until softened, then the mushrooms for 2 more minutes.
4. Add the soy sauce, salt, black pepper, and 2 cups of water; cover and bring to a boil.
5. Add the udon and cook 1-2 minutes more; drizzle with sesame oil and enjoy.
