## Ingredients

- 2 servings of udon
- 450g minced beef
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced and divided
- 100g mushroom, sliced
- chopped green onion or scallions, to serve

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Combine all the sauce ingredients in a bowl and set aside.
2. Soak the frozen udon in hot water, loosen the noodles with chopsticks, then rinse with cold water and drain.
3. Heat the oil over medium-low heat and cook the garlic, spring onion and mushroom until the mushrooms are lightly browned.
4. Add the minced beef and cook until completely browned.
5. Return the noodles to the pan, pour over the sauce and toss until evenly coated.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste, garnish with more spring onions and serve.
