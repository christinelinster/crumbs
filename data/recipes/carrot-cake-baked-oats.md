## Ingredients

- 2 bananas
- 2 cups oats
- 1 scoop or 30g protein powder
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 carrot
- 2 cups milk of choice
- Optional: cinnamon, nuts/seeds, honey

### For the yogurt frosting

- 1/2 cup greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp cream cheese
- honey/maple syrup (as much as desired)

## Instructions

1. Mash the bananas in a glass bowl, then stir in the oats, protein powder, baking powder, grated carrot, and milk.
2. Fold in the cinnamon, nuts or seeds, and a drizzle of honey if desired.
3. Pour the batter into a parchment-lined baking pan.
4. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for about 40 minutes, until the top is set and lightly golden.
5. Let cool slightly, then slice.
6. Stir the Greek yogurt, cream cheese, and honey together until smooth and spread over each slice as frosting.
