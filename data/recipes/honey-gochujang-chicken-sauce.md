## Ingredients

- chicken thighs

### For the honey gochujang sauce

- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 1-2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1-2 tbsp honey
- water to thin (optional)

## Instructions

1. Whisk the gochujang, soy sauce, rice wine vinegar, sesame oil and honey into a sauce, adding water to thin if needed.
2. Coat the chicken thighs in the sauce.
3. Cook in the air fryer around 30 minutes at 375F (or oven), watching for your preferred doneness.
4. Adjust the sauce ratios to taste.
