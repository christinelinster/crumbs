## Ingredients

- 3 egg yolks (2 for custard, 1 for egg wash)
- 60g granulated sugar
- 400ml whole milk
- 50g cornstarch
- 8g matcha powder

## Instructions

1. In a saucepan, whisk together the egg yolks, sugar, milk, cornstarch, and matcha powder until smooth and lump-free.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the mixture thickens into a custard.
3. Pour the thickened custard into a container and smooth the top; chill in the refrigerator until fully set.
4. Turn out the set custard and cut it into even slices.
5. Brush each slice with the remaining egg yolk (the egg wash).
6. Air-fry the slices until the tops are golden and slightly crisp, about 8-10 minutes at 180°C/350°F.
7. Serve warm, optionally with ice cream.
