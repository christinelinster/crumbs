## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp doenjang (Korean soybean paste)
- 1 tsp gochujang (Korean chilli paste)
- 1/2 tsp gochugaru (Korean chilli flakes, optional for heat)
- 1 cup water (200ml)
- 1/4 block tofu
- 1/4 onion
- 1/2 enoki mushroom
- 1/4 strand green onion
- 1 tsp garlic
- 1/4 zucchini
- Sliced meat (optional; pork was used)

## Instructions

1. Slice the zucchini and green onion, separate the enoki mushrooms, and cut the tofu into blocks; slice the onion.
2. Divide the vegetables and tofu among meal-prep containers; add spoonfuls of doenjang, gochujang, minced garlic, and the optional gochugaru to each.
3. Freeze the containers for up to 3-6 months.
4. To cook, empty one frozen pack into a pot and add the water and optional sliced pork.
5. Bring to a simmer and cook about 10 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the broth is hearty.
6. Serve hot.
