## Ingredients

- 12 ounces shrimp (21/25 size)
- 4 ounces ground pork (can substitute ground dark meat chicken or turkey)
- 2 tablespoons neutral oil (such as vegetable, canola, or avocado oil)
- 1 clove garlic (minced)
- 1 tablespoon clear rice wine
- 1 1/2 cups low sodium chicken stock
- 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1/4 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt (fine sea salt)
- 1/4 teaspoon MSG (optional)
- 1/8 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- 2 1/2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1 egg (beaten slightly)
- 1 scallion (chopped)


## Instructions

1. Peel and devein the shrimp, if needed. Boil 2 cups water in a wok. Add ground pork, chicken, or turkey; break it up and cook for 1 minute, until no longer pink. Drain and rinse for a clearer sauce.
2. Wash the wok and heat over medium-high. Add neutral oil, garlic, ground meat, and shrimp; stir-fry for 10-20 seconds. Add wine and cook for 10 seconds. Stir in chicken stock, sesame oil, sugar, salt, MSG if using, white pepper, and peas.
3. Bring to a simmer. Mix the cornstarch with water and add the slurry gradually, until the sauce coats a spoon. Do not let it get gloopy. Add stock if too thick or more slurry if too thin.
4. Spread the beaten egg and scallion over the sauce. Simmer for 5 seconds, then fold the egg in with a few strokes. Serve over white rice.
