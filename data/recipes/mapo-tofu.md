## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp neutral oil
- 1/2 lbs ground beef/pork
- 1 inch knob ginger, minced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 scallions (white portion sliced; green portion reserved for garnish)
- 2 tbsp doubanjiang
- 2 tsp Sichuan chili flakes
- 1-2 tsp Sichuan peppercorn powder
- 2 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 cup chicken/beef stock
- 1 lbs soft/silken tofu
- 2 tsp cornstarch + 3 tbsp water (slurry)
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Mince the ginger and garlic; slice the scallion whites for cooking and set the greens aside for garnish.
2. Cube the drained tofu and simmer it in salted water for 2-3 minutes to firm it up, then set aside.
3. Brown the ground meat in the oil over medium heat, 6-7 minutes, until its liquid evaporates and it starts to crisp.
4. Stir in the scallion whites, ginger, garlic, doubanjiang, chili flakes, and peppercorn powder; cook 3-4 minutes until fragrant.
5. Deglaze with shaoxing wine, simmer 2-3 minutes, then season with soy sauce, sugar, and white pepper.
6. Add the stock and tofu, bring to a simmer, then stir in the cornstarch slurry and mix gently until the sauce thickens.
7. Finish with sesame oil and serve over rice with the reserved scallion greens.
