## Ingredients

- 6 medium to hard boiled eggs
- 3 tbsp mayo or 1/2 avocado
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 shallot or 1/3 small red onion
- 1/3 cup fresh parsley
- black pepper, onion powder and garlic salt (to taste)
- 2 cucumbers, halved lengthwise with seeds removed

## Instructions

1. Chop the eggs, mayo (or avocado), mustard, shallot and parsley together on a cutting board until finely chopped and evenly incorporated; season to taste.
2. Fill the hollowed cucumber halves with the egg salad and serve.

## Notes

For a non-mayonnaise version, use 1/2 avocado instead.
