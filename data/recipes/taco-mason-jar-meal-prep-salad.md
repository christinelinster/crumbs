## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp light mayo
- 1 tbsp sour cream
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp taco seasoning
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- Salt to taste
- 1-2 tbsp lime juice
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 cup cooked black beans
- 1/4 cup Roma tomatoes
- Jalapeno (optional)
- 1/2 cup corn
- 1 tbsp onion
- Cilantro
- 3/4 cup cooked ground turkey
- Half an avocado
- Romaine lettuce

## Instructions

1. Add the light mayo, sour cream, olive oil, taco seasoning, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and lime juice to each mason jar and stir to make the dressing base.
2. Layer the cooked black beans over the dressing.
3. Add the corn, Roma tomatoes, onion, jalapeño (if using), and cilantro.
4. Layer in the cooked ground turkey, then the avocado, then the romaine lettuce on top.
5. Seal the jars and refrigerate; they keep for 4-5 days.
6. To serve, dump a jar into a bowl and toss everything together with the dressing.
