## Ingredients

- 1 ripe avocado (about 5.3 oz)
- 3 hard-boiled eggs
- 1/2 cup plain or Greek yogurt (4.4 oz)
- 1/2 small red onion, chopped
- chives to taste
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- spices to taste (pepper, garlic powder...)
- pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Boil the eggs, then peel them.
2. Place the avocado, eggs, yogurt, onion, chives, olive oil, spices, and salt in a bowl.
3. Mash everything well with a fork until creamy.
4. Spread into sandwiches or wraps, or serve as a healthy dip.

## Notes

The only cooking needed is boiling the eggs; the filling is otherwise no-cook.
