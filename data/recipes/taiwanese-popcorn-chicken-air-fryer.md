## Ingredients

### For the chicken

- 1 lb skinless boneless chicken thighs (raw)
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 tsp Chinese five spice powder
- 1.5 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp minced ginger
- 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 3/4 cup coarse potato starch
- 1 egg white only (no yolk)
- 1-second spray cooking oil

### For the toppings

- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp five spice powder
- 1/2 tsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 1 cup Thai basil

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken thighs into small 1-inch pieces.
2. Mix the white pepper, five spice powder, garlic, ginger, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce and egg white into a marinade; coat the chicken and rest 30 minutes (or overnight in the fridge).
3. Dredge the chicken pieces in the potato starch until fully coated.
4. Air fry on a sprayed tray at 390F for 13-15 minutes, flipping halfway, until crisp and golden; mist with more oil as needed.
5. Add the Thai basil and air fry 2-3 more minutes.
6. Mix the salt, five spice powder, brown sugar and white pepper and sprinkle over the cooked chicken before serving.
