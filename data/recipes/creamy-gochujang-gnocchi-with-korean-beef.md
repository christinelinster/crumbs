## Ingredients

- 1 cup of low fat cottage cheese
- 2 tablespoons of gochujang
- 1 tablespoon of white miso paste
- 1/4 cup of water
- 1 pound of extra lean ground beef
- 2 tablespoons each of coconut aminos, balsamic vinegar, and water
- 1 tablespoon of toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 teaspoon of black pepper
- 4 cloves of crushed garlic
- 2 teaspoons of minced ginger
- Gnocchi (quantity not stated in caption)
- Sliced green onion
- Toasted sesame seeds
- Gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese, gochujang, miso paste, and water until smooth.
2. Brown the ground beef fully, then stir in the coconut aminos, balsamic vinegar, water, sesame oil, pepper, garlic, and ginger.
3. Boil the gnocchi for a couple of minutes until they float; reserve about a cup of the water before draining.
4. Toss the gnocchi with the blended sauce and about three-quarters of the beef over low heat, loosening with reserved pasta water as needed until creamy.
5. Plate and top with the remaining beef, green onion, sesame seeds, and gochugaru.
