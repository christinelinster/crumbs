## Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 50g Greek yogurt (0% used)
- 60ml milk (almond used)
- 30g flour
- 30g protein powder (vanilla used)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- fruit of choice (blueberries, strawberries & banana used)
- honey, to drizzle

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 180C.
2. Stir all the batter ingredients together until smooth.
3. Pour into a bowl and bake about 30 minutes until set and golden.
4. Top with fruit and a drizzle of honey.
