## Ingredients

- 40g rolled oats
- 1 medium banana
- 80g high protein vanilla yoghurt / low fat greek
- 1 egg
- 20g vanilla protein powder
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 50ml almond milk (or milk of choice)
- 15g chocolate chips (reduced sugar)
- optional: vanilla essence, sweetener

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients together in an oven-safe bowl.
2. Bake 40 minutes at 180C/350F, or 30 minutes in the air fryer.
