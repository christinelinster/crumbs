## Ingredients

- 1 medium onion
- 1 stalk celery
- 1 medium carrot
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp olive oil (total, for paste and garlic)
- 1 28oz can San Marzano tomatoes
- 1 handful fresh parsley and basil, chopped
- 8oz (224g) rigatoni
- 6 cups water (for pasta)
- 10oz chicken tenderloins, cut into cubes
- 50g 1/3 fat cream cheese
- 30g parmigiano reggiano or romano

## Instructions

1. Sweat the chopped onion, celery, and carrot in olive oil over medium heat for about 10 minutes until translucent.
2. Push the vegetables aside and cook the tomato paste in olive oil for 2 minutes, then add the garlic and cook 2-3 minutes more before mixing everything together.
3. Add the canned tomatoes, break them up, and simmer with the herbs on medium-low until most of the liquid cooks off; blend until smooth.
4. Cook the rigatoni in 6 cups of salted water.
5. Cook the cubed chicken until tender and pour the sauce over it.
6. Combine the pasta with the sauce and about 1/2 cup of reserved pasta water, keeping it over medium-low heat until thickened.
7. Stir in the cream cheese and parmigiano, let rest a few minutes, and serve.
