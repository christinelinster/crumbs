## Ingredients

- 28oz boneless skinless chicken thighs, diced
- 80g buffalo sauce
- 30g Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon
- 200ml water
- 672g pasta, undercooked by 50% of package time
- 600g 2% blended cottage cheese
- 150g 1/3 fat cream cheese
- 40g parmigiano reggiano
- 50g cheddar powder
- 120g buffalo sauce
- 60g honey
- 300ml milk
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Slow cook the chicken with the buffalo sauce, Worcestershire, spices, bouillon, and water on High 3-4 hours or Low 6-8 hours.
2. Stir in the half-cooked pasta along with the blended cheeses, extra buffalo sauce, honey, milk, salt, and pepper (add 100-200ml water if too thick).
3. Cook 15-20 minutes more until the pasta is done.
4. Divide into 10 equal containers, freeze, and microwave about 4 minutes to reheat.
