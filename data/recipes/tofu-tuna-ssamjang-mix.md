## Ingredients

- 1 block of extra firm or firm tofu (1lb)
- 2 cans of tuna, oil drained
- 1/2 cup of chopped peppers (any kind; bell pepper for non-spicy, jalapeño for a kick)
- 4 scallions, chopped
- 2 tsp of sesame oil
- 1 tsp perilla oil (optional)
- 1/2 - 1 cup ssamjang (to taste)

## Instructions

1. Drain the two cans of tuna.
2. Chop the block of extra-firm tofu into small pieces on a cutting board.
3. Add the tuna, tofu, chopped peppers, and chopped scallions to a large mixing bowl.
4. Add the ssamjang (1/2 to 1 cup, to taste), sesame oil, and perilla oil if using.
5. Mix everything thoroughly with a wooden spoon until well combined.
6. Serve wrapped in lettuce leaves; pack leftovers into small containers and refrigerate.
