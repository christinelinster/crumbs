## Ingredients

- 6oz chicken breast, skinless, boneless, raw
- 1/2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/3 second cooking spray
- 3oz silken tofu
- 1 tbsp gochujang paste
- salt & pepper to taste
- 1/2 persian cucumber
- 1/4 yellow onion
- 1 green onion
- 200g japanese udon noodles

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumber, yellow onion, and green onions thinly.
2. Cut the chicken into half-inch cubes.
3. Toss the chicken with the garlic and soy sauce and set aside.
4. Blend the tofu, gochujang, salt, and pepper into a sauce, keeping it a bit thick.
5. Sear the chicken in a spray-oiled pan over medium heat.
6. Boil the noodles, then shock them in cold water.
7. Bowl up the noodles, spoon over the sauce, and finish with the chicken and vegetables.
