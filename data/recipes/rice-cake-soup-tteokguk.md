## Ingredients

- 800g rice cake
- 150g beef
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 cups water
- 2 cups bone broth
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce for soup
- salt to taste
- pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Combine the rice cake, beef, water, bone broth, and sesame oil in a pot.
2. Season with the soup soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
3. Simmer until everything is cooked through and serve.
