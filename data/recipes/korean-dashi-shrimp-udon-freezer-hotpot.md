## Ingredients

- 200g shrimp
- 100g udon
- any korean dashi soup packet
- 100g white radish
- 80g enoki mushrooms

## Instructions

1. Pack all the ingredients into a freezer bag for one pot; freeze.
2. To serve: add water and boil.
