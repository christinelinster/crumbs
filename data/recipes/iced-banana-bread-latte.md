## Ingredients

- 2 tsp instant coffee
- 2 pumps vanilla syrup (sugar free, 0 cal)
- 1 tbsp warm water
- 2-4 tbsp homemade banana bread syrup
- 1/2 cup milk
- ice

### For the banana bread syrup

- 2 bananas, frozen
- 1/4 cup Swerve brown sugar
- 1/2 cup coconut sugar
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 cup water
- 1/2 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Make the banana bread syrup by simmering the bananas, sugars, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt and water together, then blending with a hand whisk.
2. Dissolve the instant coffee and vanilla syrup in the warm water.
3. Add 2-4 tbsp of banana bread syrup and stir.
4. Fill a glass with ice, pour in 1/2 cup of milk, and enjoy.
