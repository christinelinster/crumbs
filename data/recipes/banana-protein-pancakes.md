## Ingredients

- 1/2 - 1 banana
- 1/4 cup oats
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 scoop protein powder
- 2 tbsp almond milk (more if needed)
- 1 egg
- 1 - 2 tbsp PB2
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Heat a pan on medium heat and grease it with cooking oil or spray.
2. Pour batter onto the hot pan.
3. Wait until bubbles form and the bottom browns, then flip.
4. Plate and top generously with fruit, peanut butter, and maple syrup.

## Notes

These pancakes also work without protein powder.
