## Ingredients

- 1.25 lbs Thinly sliced brisket
- 1/2 Medium onion, sliced
- 1 Knob ginger, sliced
- 0.5 Cup Dashi stock
- 5 tbsp Soy sauce
- 5 tbsp Mirin
- 5 tsp Monk fruit sweetener (or sugar)

## Instructions

1. Simmer the dashi, soy sauce, mirin, monk fruit sweetener, and ginger in a wide pan.
2. Add the onions and simmer until they begin to soften.
3. Add the brisket slices and simmer 3-5 minutes until just tender.
4. Lift out the beef and onions and set aside.
5. Add the napa cabbage to the remaining sauce, cover, and steam/simmer until soft and flavorful.
6. Assemble: layer 1/2 cup rice, then cabbage, then beef; top with a 7-minute soft-boiled egg.

## Notes

Napa cabbage is mentioned in the steps but is not included in the ingredient list.
