## Ingredients

- 500g boneless/skinless chicken breast, cut into 1 inch cubes
- 4 tbsp cooking oil, divided
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, minced
- 10 dried chilies, cut into 2 cm pieces
- 2 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorns
- 4 green onions, white and green parts, cut into 2 inch pieces
- 1/3 cup roasted/unsalted peanuts

### For the marinade

- 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- pinch of salt and pepper

### For the sauce

- 1/2 cup chicken stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp cornstarch

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken and marinate with cooking wine, soy sauce, baking soda, cornstarch, salt and pepper for 15 minutes.
2. Combine all the sauce ingredients in a bowl and set aside.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil and fry the peanuts about 1 minute until golden; set aside.
4. Heat the remaining 3 tbsp oil and cook the chicken about 5 minutes; set aside.
5. In the same pan, briefly fry the garlic, ginger and dried chilies until fragrant, about 20 seconds.
6. Return the chicken with the sauce, onion whites and Sichuan pepper; cook 1 minute until the sauce thickens.
7. Stir in the green onions and peanuts for 30 seconds, then serve over rice.

## Notes

Serve with rice.
