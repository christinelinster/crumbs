## Ingredients

- 1 chicken thigh
- 3 splashes shaoxing wine (can substitute dry sherry)
- splash of sesame oil
- splash of white pepper
- cabbage (quantity not specified)
- ginger, garlic, scallions (quantities not specified)
- hot oil (avocado or olive oil preferred, quantity not specified)
- salt (quantity not specified)

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken thigh into slices and marinate with shaoxing wine, sesame oil and white pepper for at least 30 minutes.
2. Place the cabbage and chicken on a plate and steam 10-15 minutes over medium heat.
3. Mince the ginger, garlic and scallions, pour hot oil over them, and spoon over the steamed chicken.
4. Season with salt and serve.
