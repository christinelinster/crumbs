## Ingredients

- 120g (1/2 cup) Greek yoghurt
- 60g (1/2 cup) plain flour
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp erythritol sweetener + 1 tbsp coconut sugar to sprinkle on top
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- Half a banana, chopped into pieces

## Instructions

1. Mix everything except the banana in a bowl.
2. Spoon dough balls into an oven-proof dish with two spoons.
3. Fill with banana pieces and sprinkle coconut sugar over the top.
4. Bake or air fry at 170°C for about 20 minutes.

## Notes

For the protein-powder swap, reduce the flour by 1 tablespoon.
