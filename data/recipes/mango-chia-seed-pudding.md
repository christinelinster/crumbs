## Ingredients

- 2 large champagne mangoes
- 1/2 cup vanilla yogurt
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3 tbsp chia seeds for loose pudding + 1 extra tbsp for thicker pudding

## Instructions

1. Blend the mango, yogurt, coconut milk and vanilla until completely smooth.
2. Stir the chia seeds in thoroughly.
3. Chill overnight (or at least a few hours) to thicken.
4. Serve topped with extra mango chunks, nuts and/or desiccated coconut.

## Notes

Adjust the chia seeds to get your preferred pudding thickness.
