## Ingredients

- 6 large eggs
- 1/2 cup diced yellow onion
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 2 cups kale
- 1/2 cup sun dried tomatoes (in oil; rinsed and patted dry)
- 1/2 cup cottage cheese
- 3/4 cup almond flour
- 1/4 cup coconut flour (can sub 3/4 cup more almond flour)
- 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 cup shredded cheese
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Saute the onion, garlic, and kale in avocado oil until the onion softens and the kale wilts.
3. Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper, then stir in the cottage cheese, kale mixture, flours, and baking powder into a thick batter; let rest 5 minutes.
4. Scoop 1/4-cup portions onto the sheet and sprinkle each with shredded cheese.
5. Bake 18-25 minutes until golden and firm.
6. Refrigerate 4-5 days and reheat as desired.
