## Ingredients

- 48oz skirt steak, exterior fat trimmed
- 5 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp gochujang
- 3 tbsp mirin
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp garlic puree
- 1 tbsp ginger puree
- black pepper to taste
- 4 tbsp oyster sauce (add after cooking)
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil (add after cooking)
- 600g rice, uncooked weight
- sesame seeds and green onions, to garnish

### For the spicy cream sauce

- 200g nonfat skyr or Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tsp chili crisp
- 40ml milk, to desired consistency
- salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Trim the steak and combine it with the soy sauce, gochujang, mirin, honey, garlic, and ginger purees, and black pepper in the slow cooker.
2. Cook on low about 6 hours or on high about 4 hours.
3. Shred the beef, then stir in the oyster sauce and sesame oil.
4. Whisk the spicy cream sauce ingredients together, thinning with milk to your liking.
5. Assemble bowls with rice, beef, cream sauce, sesame seeds, and green onions.
