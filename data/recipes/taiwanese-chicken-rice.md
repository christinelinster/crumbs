## Ingredients

- 1 pack chicken breast or thigh
- white pepper
- 1 cup chicken broth
- sliced ginger and garlic
- generous amount of five spice seasoning
- shallots
- oil (for frying shallots)

## Instructions

1. Place the chicken in an Instant Pot with the chicken broth, white pepper, sliced ginger and garlic, and a generous amount of five spice seasoning.
2. Pressure cook on high for 15 minutes.
3. Thinly slice the shallots and fry them in oil until crisp.
4. Slice or shred the cooked chicken and serve over rice with cucumbers, extra white pepper, and the fried shallots.
