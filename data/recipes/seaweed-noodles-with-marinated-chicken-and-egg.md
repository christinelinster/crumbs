## Ingredients

- Haechomin seaweed noodles (cold noodle pack)
- chicken breast, 80-150g raw
- 1 egg
- corn kernels, 20g
- cucumber
- carrots
- salt and pepper (to taste)

### For the chicken marinade

- stevia
- 1/2 tomato, crushed
- garlic powder
- salt
- pepper
- chili powder

## Instructions

1. Marinate the chicken in the stevia, crushed tomato, garlic powder, salt, pepper and chili powder; oven-bake until cooked through.
2. Boil the egg for 6 minutes, chill in an ice bath, then peel.
3. Assemble the seaweed noodles with their sauce, sliced chicken, egg, corn, cucumber and carrots; season with salt and pepper if needed.
