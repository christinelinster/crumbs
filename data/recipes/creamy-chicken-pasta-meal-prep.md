## Ingredients

- 150g chicken (per serving)
- 40g pasta (per serving)
- 100g mixed peppers (per serving)
- 1/3 of a medium red onion (per serving)
- 1 tsp paprika (chicken seasoning)
- 1 tsp garlic (chicken seasoning)
- salt and pepper (chicken seasoning)

### For the sauce

- 1 tin chopped tomatoes, blended (sauce)
- 5g tomato puree (sauce)
- 20g lightest Philadelphia (sauce)
- 20g Greek-style yoghurt (sauce)

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with paprika, garlic, salt and pepper and cook through.
2. Cook the pasta and chop/cook the peppers and onion.
3. Blend the tin of chopped tomatoes with the puree, Philadelphia and Greek yoghurt to make the sauce.
4. Combine the cooked chicken, pasta, peppers and onion with the sauce.
