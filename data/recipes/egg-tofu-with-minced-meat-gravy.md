## Ingredients

- 1 tofu
- 2 eggs
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- Minced meat (pork/beef/chicken up to u)
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/2 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 tbsp oyster sauce
- A pinch of ground pepper
- 3-4 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Heat the cooking oil in a pan, then lay in the log of egg tofu and slice it into rounds.
2. Beat the eggs and pour them over the tofu; cover and cook until the egg is set.
3. In a separate pan, heat the remaining oil and stir-fry the minced meat with the minced garlic and grated ginger until browned.
4. Add the light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, sugar, oyster sauce, pepper, and water; simmer briefly to form a savory gravy.
5. Pour the hot meat gravy over the egg-tofu mixture in the pan.
6. Serve steaming hot over white rice.
