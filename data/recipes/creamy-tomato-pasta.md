## Ingredients

- 16-20oz cottage cheese (blended till smooth)
- 1/3 cup extra virgin olive oil
- 1 (6oz can) tomato paste
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp minced shallots
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 cup reserved pasta water (use ~3/4 cup)
- 1/3 cup fresh basil
- 1/3 cup freshly shredded parmesan cheese
- red pepper flakes (optional)
- 16 oz rigatoni

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the rigatoni to al dente; reserve 1 cup of pasta water before draining.
2. Warm the olive oil in a saucepan over medium heat; cook the shallots, garlic, and tomato paste for 4-5 minutes, stirring continuously, until the paste darkens to a reddish brown.
3. Lower the heat, stir in the blended cottage cheese until incorporated, and simmer for 2 minutes.
4. Stir in the red pepper flakes, basil, salt, and pepper.
5. Mix in the parmesan, butter, and 3/4 cup of the reserved pasta water; add more water as needed for the desired consistency.
6. Serve topped with extra cheese, red pepper flakes, and basil; sliced grilled chicken is an optional topping.

## Notes

Lower the heat before stirring in the cottage cheese to keep it from separating.
