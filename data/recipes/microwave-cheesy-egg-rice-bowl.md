## Ingredients

- 1 cup cold, cooked white rice
- 2 large eggs
- 3 ounces Spam luncheon meat
- 2 medium scallions
- 2 teaspoons soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 cup drained kimchi
- 1/2 cup low-moisture shredded mozzarella cheese
- Korean seaweed (gim), for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Place the rice in a microwave-safe bowl, crack in the eggs and stir so every grain is coated.
2. Dice the Spam, slice the scallions (reserve 1 tbsp of the dark green parts), and stir both into the rice with the soy sauce and sesame oil.
3. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap (leaving a small vent) and microwave about 3 minutes, stirring halfway, until the eggs are nearly set.
4. Uncover, top with kimchi and mozzarella, and microwave uncovered about 1 minute 15 seconds until the cheese melts and eggs finish setting.
5. Sprinkle with the reserved scallion greens and serve with Korean seaweed if desired.

## Notes

Prepare the bowl entirely in the microwave; kimchi and seaweed are optional garnishes.
