## Ingredients

- 1 cup oats (quick or old fashioned)
- 3 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder (plus more to top)
- 1.5 tbsp chia seeds
- 3 tbsp espresso or brewed coffee (cooled)
- 1.25 cups unsweetened almond milk
- 2.5 tbsp pure maple syrup or honey
- 1.25 tsp vanilla extract
- 1.5 cup Greek yogurt

## Instructions

1. Stir the oats, cocoa, and chia together; add the espresso, almond milk, 2 tbsp of the sweetener, and 1 tsp of the vanilla; mix well and refrigerate 4-8 hours or overnight.
2. Stir the remaining sweetener and vanilla into the Greek yogurt.
3. Layer half the oats in a bowl with 3/4 cup of the yogurt and dust with cocoa.
4. Repeat for the second serving.

## Notes

Reserve a little sweetener and vanilla for the yogurt topping.
