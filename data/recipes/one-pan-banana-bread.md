## Ingredients

- 3 ripe bananas
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp baking powder
- splash of vanilla

## Instructions

1. Mix all the ingredients together in a greased bread pan.
2. Bake at 400F for 30 minutes.
3. Let cool, slice, and enjoy.
