## Ingredients

- 1 onion (minced)
- 1/2 large carrot (minced)
- 4-5 cloves garlic
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1.5T soy sauce (marinade)
- 1t sugar (marinade)
- 1t cornstarch (marinade)
- 2-3T tomato paste
- 5-6T ketchup
- 2T soy sauce
- 3-4 bay leaves
- 3 cups chicken broth (or 3 cups water with 2-3t chicken bouillon powder)
- 3-4t sugar (or to taste)
- salt to taste (if needed)
- 1 egg per serving, pan-fried sunny side up
- rice or spaghetti, for serving

## Instructions

1. Marinate the ground beef with soy sauce, sugar and cornstarch and set aside.
2. Chop the onion, carrot and garlic.
3. Heat oil in a medium/large pan over medium heat; saute the onion and garlic until translucent, then add the carrot until slightly softened.
4. Add the marinated beef along with the ketchup, soy sauce and tomato paste; stir 1-2 minutes until well combined.
5. Pour in the broth, bring to a boil, then simmer covered on low for 45 minutes.
6. Reduce over high heat to thicken, adjusting salt and sugar; use a cornstarch slurry if needed.
7. Pan-fry a sunny side up egg per serving and serve the sauce over rice or spaghetti.

## Notes

Serve over rice or spaghetti.
