## Ingredients

- 1 cup pork belly, cut into small pieces
- 1/2 cup kimchi, chopped
- 1 package silken soft tofu
- 1/2 package enoki mushrooms
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoon garlic
- 1-2 tablespoon Korean hot pepper flakes (gochugaru)
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 tablespoon sugar
- 1/2 cup kimchi juice
- 1.5 cups water
- 1 stalk green onions, finely chopped

## Instructions

1. Warm a small stew pot over medium-high heat with a little oil and saute the garlic.
2. Add the pork belly on high heat and brown it until caramelized, then stir in the chopped kimchi.
3. Mix in the gochugaru, salt, sugar, and sesame oil.
4. Pour in the kimchi juice and water; bring to a boil, about 2 minutes.
5. Add the tofu, eggs, and enoki mushrooms and cook 2-3 more minutes.
6. Top with green onions and serve with white rice and extra kimchi.
