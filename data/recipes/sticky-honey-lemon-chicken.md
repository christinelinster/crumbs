## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1 tablespoon cumin
- 1 tablespoon oregano
- lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 30g light butter
- 1 tablespoon garlic
- 1 teaspoon oregano
- lemon juice
- 70ml light soy sauce
- 15g honey
- parsley
- 200g uncooked washed rice

## Instructions

1. Dice the chicken into cubes and season with garlic powder, cumin, oregano, lemon juice and olive oil.
2. Cook the chicken on a pan over high heat for 10-15 minutes until browned (or bake/air fry 15 minutes at 220C).
3. Set the chicken aside; melt the light butter in the pan over medium heat and add garlic, oregano and lemon juice.
4. Stir in the light soy sauce and honey, return the chicken, and simmer about 10 minutes until the sauce turns sticky.
5. Cook the rice (200g uncooked yields about 630g cooked), garnish with parsley, and divide into 5 containers.
