## Ingredients

- 4 cups kimchi
- 1 1/2 cups flour
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 jalapeno, diced
- pinch of salt (to taste)
- 1 cup green onion
- 10 oz light canned tuna in water
- 1 tbsp gochugaru
- 1-second spray oil

## Instructions

1. Finely slice the green onions and jalapeno.
2. Mix the green onions, jalapeno, kimchi, flour, water, tuna, gochugaru and salt in a large bowl to a batter-like consistency.
3. Spread the batter thin in a sprayed pan over medium heat and cook until the bottom crisps and the pancake slides freely when shaken.
4. Flip using a paper plate and cook the other side until browned and crisp on both sides.

## Notes

The batter makes around 2-4 jeon, depending on pan size.
