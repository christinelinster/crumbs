## Ingredients

- 5 egg whites
- 40g grated Parmesan
- salt (to taste)
- pepper (to taste)
- onion powder (to taste)
- garlic powder (to taste)
- mixed herbs (to taste)

## Instructions

1. Combine the egg whites with the Parmesan and seasonings.
2. Spread the mixture into thin rounds on a lined baking tray.
3. Bake at 200C for 10-13 minutes until crisp and golden.
4. Cool briefly and serve, optionally with a low-calorie dip such as tzatziki.
