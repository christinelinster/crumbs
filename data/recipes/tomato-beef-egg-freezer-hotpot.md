## Ingredients

- 200g lean beef slices
- 2 eggs (add fresh when cooking)
- diy tomato soup block
- 100g napa cabbage
- 80g enoki mushrooms
- 50g glass noodles

## Instructions

1. Pack all the ingredients except the eggs into a freezer bag for one pot; freeze.
2. To serve: add water and boil, adding the eggs fresh when cooking.

## Notes

Keep the eggs fresh and add them when reheating; do not freeze them in the pack.
