## Ingredients

- Rice paper sheets
- Water, for soaking
- Oil
- Seasoning of choice

## Instructions

1. Soften one rice paper sheet in water until pliable, then roll it up tightly from the top.
2. Repeat with a few more sheets to make several rolls.
3. Lay the rolls on a plate or tray (avoid loose parchment paper in the air fryer).
4. Cut the rolls into small pieces.
5. Brush the pieces lightly with oil.
6. Air fry at 190C for about 12 minutes, until puffed and crisp.
7. Toss with your chosen seasoning until evenly coated.
