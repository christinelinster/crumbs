## Ingredients

- chicken (amount not stated)
- 2oz soy sauce
- 2oz mirin
- 2oz cooking sake
- minced garlic & ginger, optional
- cooked rice, to serve
- optional toppings: kimchi, green onions, sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Mix equal parts soy sauce, mirin, and sake with optional garlic and ginger.
2. Bring the sauce to a gentle boil and reduce about 5 minutes until slightly thicker and glossy.
3. Hard-sear the chicken on both sides, then lower to medium heat.
4. Pour in three-quarters of the sauce, cover, and braise until cooked through.
5. Flip, add the remaining sauce, and cook to your preferred thickness, adding a splash of water if needed.
6. Serve over rice with kimchi, green onions, and sesame seeds.
