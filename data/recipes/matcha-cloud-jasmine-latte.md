## Ingredients

- 1.5 tsp matcha
- 1 tsp hot water
- 1/3 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 tsp liquid cane sugar
- 3/4 cup jasmine-infused milk

## Instructions

1. Whisk the matcha with the hot water until completely smooth.
2. Froth or whip the heavy cream with the liquid cane sugar until thickened into a foam cloud.
3. Pour the jasmine-infused milk into a glass and spoon the matcha cream layer over the top, then serve.
