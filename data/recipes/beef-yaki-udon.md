## Ingredients

- 2 servings of udon (frozen used)
- 225g sliced beef
- 1 tbsp butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 4 stalks green onion, cut into 2-inch pieces (white and green parts separated)
- Sesame seeds, green onion, and nori seaweed, to garnish

### For the sauce

- 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp rice wine vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp water

## Instructions

1. Whisk all sauce ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Loosen the frozen udon in hot water with chopsticks, then rinse under cold water and drain.
3. Melt the butter over medium heat and soften the brown onion, garlic, and white parts of the green onion for about 3 minutes.
4. Add the beef and cook about 2 minutes until browned.
5. Return the noodles to the pan, pour in the sauce, and toss to coat evenly.
6. Garnish with sesame seeds, green onion, and nori.
