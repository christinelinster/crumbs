## Ingredients

### For the meat
- salt
- pepper
- garlic powder
- paprika powder
- olive oil
- minced garlic
- green onion
### For the sauce
- 250g silken tofu
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- pinch of salt
- Fly by Jing noodles

## Instructions

1. Place the silken tofu in a blender cup with the gochujang, sesame oil, and a pinch of salt; blend until smooth and orange.
2. Season the diced chicken (or chosen meat) with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika; cook in a hot pan with a little olive oil until cooked through, then remove from the pan.
3. In the remaining oil, sauté the minced garlic and chopped green onion with an extra pinch of paprika until fragrant, then return the chicken to the pan.
4. Cook the noodles according to the package directions and drain.
5. Place the noodles in a bowl, top with the chicken mixture, and pour the blended tofu-gochujang sauce over everything.
6. Mix well and serve.
