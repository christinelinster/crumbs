## Ingredients

- 1/2 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp vanilla bean paste
- double shot of espresso
- ice
- 3-4oz milk

### For the honey cold foam

- 2-3 tbsp heavy cream
- 1 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp honey

## Instructions

1. Stir the honey and vanilla bean paste into the espresso shots.
2. Add ice and the milk.
3. Whip the heavy cream, milk and honey until foamy and pour over the top.
