## Ingredients

- onions and garlic (quantities not specified)
- butter (quantity not specified)
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2/3 cup (4 tbsp) cooking cream
- cooked udon (quantity not specified)
- spring onions, cheese, kimchi for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook the onions and garlic in butter until fragrant.
2. Stir in the gochujang until combined.
3. Pour in the cooking cream and let it cook together.
4. Add the cooked udon and mix well (cook frozen udon 1-2 minutes first).
5. Garnish optionally with spring onions, cheese and kimchi and serve.
