## Ingredients

- 20oz diced chicken breast
- 35g soy sauce
- 25g sriracha
- 10g lime juice
- garlic powder, onion powder, red chili flakes (to taste)
- 10 cloves minced garlic
- 5 green onions (whites only, reserve greens for garnish)
- 100g creamy peanut butter
- 40g soy sauce
- 20g sriracha
- 75g honey
- 40g sundried tomatoes
- 2-3 tablespoons lime juice
- 300g pasta or noodles of choice

## Instructions

1. Toss the diced chicken with soy sauce, sriracha, lime juice and the dry seasonings.
2. Sear the chicken over high heat for 3-4 minutes, then lower the heat and stir in the minced garlic and green onion whites.
3. Whisk together the peanut butter, soy sauce, sriracha, honey, sundried tomatoes and lime juice to make the sauce.
4. Cook the pasta, reserving 1/3 cup of the pasta water when nearly done.
5. Remove the chicken from the pan, add the peanut sauce and thin it with the reserved pasta water over low heat, then toss with the cooked noodles.
6. Return the chicken to the pan, combine, and portion into 5 even servings.
