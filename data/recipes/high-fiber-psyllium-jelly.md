## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp psyllium husk
- 8-12 oz tart cherry juice

## Instructions

1. Add the psyllium husk to the tart cherry juice and shake well.
2. Let it sit 5-10 minutes until jelly-like.
3. Drink or eat with a spoon.
