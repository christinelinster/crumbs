## Ingredients

### Main ingredients
- 14 oz uncooked jasmine rice
- 14 oz water
- 1 oz green onion
- 2 oz carrot
- 4 oz frozen corn and peas
- 4 large egg
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 0.3 cup water
- 2 tablespoon oil
### Rice seasoning ingredients
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon dark soy sauce
- 0.5 teaspoon salt
- 0.5 teaspoon sugar
- 1 teaspoon chicken bouillon powder
- 1 tablespoon oil


## Instructions

1. Rinse the rice three times, using fresh water each time. Drain well.
2. Dice the green parts of the scallions. Save the whites for another dish.
3. Crack the eggs into a bowl, season with salt, and set aside.
4. After the rice finishes cooking, leave it covered for 2 minutes to steam.
5. Heat a wok over high heat. Beat the eggs, reduce the heat to low, and add the oil, coating the wok.
6. Plate the fried rice and serve.
