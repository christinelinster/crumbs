## Ingredients

- 2oz soy sauce
- 1oz oyster sauce
- 1oz mirin
- honey
- garlic
- 1/2 onion, sliced
- cooked rice
- beef slices
- corn
- green onions
- butter
- black pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the soy sauce, oyster sauce, mirin, honey, and garlic into a sauce.
2. Cook the sliced onion about 1-2 minutes, then push to the center.
3. Add rice on top, then the beef slices, corn, green onions, and butter.
4. Pour over the sauce, finish with black pepper, and mix everything together.
