## Ingredients

- 1lb 90/10 lean ground beef
- 2.5 tbsp garlic minced
- 1 tbsp ginger minced
- 2 tbsp doubanjiang (spicy bean paste)
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp Sichuan peppercorn
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- 1.5 tbsp cornstarch
- 6 tbsp water
- 15 oz silken tofu
- 1 green onion

## Instructions

1. Thinly slice the green onion.
2. Cook the ground beef in a pan over medium heat, seasoning with salt and pepper to taste.
3. Stir in the garlic, ginger, oyster sauce, doubanjiang, soy sauce, Sichuan peppercorn, and chicken bouillon powder.
4. Mix the cornstarch with the water into a slurry and slowly pour it into the pan while stirring.
5. Score the silken tofu into small cubes with a knife and add to the pan, stirring gently until fully incorporated.
6. Top with green onions and serve.

## Notes

Serve with rice.
