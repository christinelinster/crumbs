## Ingredients

- 5 shiitake mushrooms, soaked and sliced
- 1 carrot, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup bean sprouts
- 1/2 cup spinach
- 1 cup short grain rice + 1 cup mushroom dashi (or water)
- 1 steak, sliced (1/2 lb)
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 3 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 finely minced garlic

### For the bibim sauce

- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 garlic, grated or finely minced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Toss the sliced steak with the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and minced garlic to marinate.
2. Stir the gochujang, sugar, sesame oil, rice vinegar, garlic, and sesame seeds into the bibim sauce.
3. Load the rice, dashi, mushrooms, carrot, bean sprouts, spinach, and marinated steak into the rice cooker.
4. Cook through, then serve with the bibim sauce stirred in.
