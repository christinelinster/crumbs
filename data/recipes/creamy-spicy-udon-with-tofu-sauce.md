## Ingredients

- udon or noodle of choice
- 300g silken tofu
- 2 tbsp peanut butter (or any nut butter)
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp chilli oil
- green onion
- 100-120g ground pork (or beef/chicken)
- 1 tsp gochujang
- 1/2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp honey (or sugar/maple syrup)

## Instructions

1. Blend the tofu, peanut butter, soy sauce, sesame oil, and garlic into a smooth cream sauce.
2. Sizzle the chilli oil with garlic and green onion until fragrant, then brown the minced pork.
3. Stir the gochujang, honey, and soy sauce through the pork until well seasoned.
4. Serve over cooked noodles, pour over the tofu sauce, and finish with green onion and chilli oil.
