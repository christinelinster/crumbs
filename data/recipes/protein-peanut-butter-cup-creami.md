## Ingredients

- 1 (13.5oz) can lite coconut milk
- 1 scoop (1/3 cup) vanilla protein powder
- 1/4 cup powdered peanut butter
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/8 tsp kosher sea salt
- 1/2 cup dairy-free chocolate peanut butter cups, halved

## Instructions

1. Blend everything except the peanut butter cups until no lumps remain, then pour into the Ninja Creami container and freeze overnight.
2. Run the frozen container on the 'Ice Cream' setting.
3. Add 1 tbsp nut milk and 'Respin', repeating the respin process twice until smooth and creamy.
4. Stir in the peanut butter cups and run the 'Mix-ins' cycle.
5. Serve with extra peanut butter cups on top.

## Notes

Requires a Ninja Creami.
