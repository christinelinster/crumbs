## Ingredients

- 28oz chicken breast
- 4 tbsp adobo sauce
- 4-5 minced chipotle peppers
- 2 tsp garlic
- 2 tsp onion
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon
- 300ml water
- 672g pasta, cooked to 50%
- ~1/2 cup pasta water
- 600g 2% blended cottage cheese
- 120g 1/3 fat cream cheese
- 50g parmigiano reggiano
- 50g mozzarella
- 50g honey
- 300ml milk
- salt and pepper to taste
- chives (for topping)

## Instructions

1. Slow cook the chicken with the adobo sauce, chipotle peppers, seasonings, bouillon, and water on High 2-3 hours or Low 5-6 hours.
2. Stir in the half-cooked pasta, some pasta water, and the blended cheese sauce with honey and milk.
3. Cook 15-20 minutes more.
4. Top with chives, divide into 10 containers, freeze, and microwave about 4 minutes to reheat.
