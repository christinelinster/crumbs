## Ingredients

- 1 block silken tofu
- 2 eggs
- chopped green onions
- white sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp chili oil or chili sauce
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 4 cloves minced garlic

## Instructions

1. Beat the eggs and mix all the sauce ingredients in a separate bowl.
2. Heat oil in a pan, carefully add the tofu (it may splatter), then pour the beaten eggs around it.
3. Score the tofu lightly so it soaks up flavor.
4. Pour in the sauce, cover, and simmer on low until the eggs are set, shaking the pan or nudging the eggs to cook evenly.
5. Finish with green onions and sesame seeds and serve hot.
