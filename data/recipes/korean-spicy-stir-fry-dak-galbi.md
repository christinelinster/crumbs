## Ingredients

- 400g chicken thighs
- 45g low fat mozzarella cheese
- 60g rice cake
- 200g Japanese sweet potato
- 400g cabbage
- 1 medium yellow onion
- chopped Chinese leeks (or green onions)
- green onion

### For the marinade

- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp gochugaru (adjust to spice tolerance)
- 4 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp garlic
- 2 tbsp brown stevia
- 1 tsp salt & pepper

## Instructions

1. Whisk the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, oyster sauce, garlic, stevia, salt, and pepper into a marinade.
2. Cube the chicken thighs and coat in the marinade.
3. Add everything except the cheese and green onions to a lightly oiled large pan over medium-high heat.
4. Splash in a little water to help the vegetables soften, stirring occasionally until the sweet potato is tender.
5. Top with mozzarella and green onions and serve.
