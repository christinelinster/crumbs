## Ingredients

- 3 spotty bananas
- 2 room temp eggs
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1/3 cup maple syrup (or date syrup)
- 3 tbsp butter or coconut oil
- 2 cups blanched almond flour
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 tsp baking powder
- pinch of salt
- chocolate chips of choice

## Instructions

1. Combine the ingredients into a smooth batter.
2. Transfer to a baking vessel and bake at 350F for 45-50 minutes (45 for a gooier center; add more time if needed).
