## Ingredients

- 1/4 yellow onion, finely chopped
- handful of baby spinach, chopped
- pinch of salt
- 1/2 cup cottage cheese
- 2 tbsp parmesan, grated
- 1 large egg
- 3 rice paper sheets
- optional: 75g ground chicken for extra protein

## Instructions

1. Saute the onion until soft; add the spinach and salt and cook until wilted; drain off as much water as possible in a strainer.
2. Mix the cottage cheese, parmesan, and spinach mixture; stir in the cooked chicken if using.
3. Whisk the egg in a shallow bowl; dip each rice paper sheet and slightly overlap them on a board.
4. Spread the filling in a thin line across the lower half; let the rice paper soften 1-2 minutes.
5. Roll tightly, fold in the sides, and keep rolling into a long log; coil into a spiral.
6. Air fry at 400F for 10-12 minutes until crispy and golden.
