## Ingredients

- 1 roasted Korean sweet potato
- 200 mL cold unsweetened soy milk
- 1/4 tsp cinnamon (optional)

## Instructions

1. Peel the roasted sweet potato.
2. Blend the sweet potato with the cold soy milk and cinnamon until thick and smooth.
3. Spoon over a drink of choice, or enjoy on its own.

## Notes

Korean sweet potatoes and cold milk give the best texture.
