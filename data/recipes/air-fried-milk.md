## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 7 tbsp cornstarch
- 4 tbsp sugar (or more or less to preference)
- 1 beaten egg
- cornstarch (for breading)
- panko (for breading)
- oil for spraying

## Instructions

1. Whisk the milk, cornstarch and sugar in a saucepan until smooth and the sugar dissolves.
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, 5-7 minutes until thickened.
3. Transfer to a lightly greased container, level it, cover loosely and refrigerate until fully set.
4. Turn the chilled slab out onto a cutting board and cut into 10 equal rectangular logs.
5. Coat each log in cornstarch (shake off excess), dip in beaten egg, then coat in panko, packing it on gently.
6. Arrange in the air fryer basket, spray generously with oil, and air fry at 400F for 8-10 minutes until golden and crispy.
7. Flip halfway and spray with oil again.
8. Cool a few minutes and serve warm, with condensed milk if desired.
