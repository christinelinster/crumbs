## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup cottage cheese (full fat recommended)
- 1 egg white
- spices of choice
- toppings of choice: runny eggs, avocado, chives, hot sauce

## Instructions

1. Whisk the cottage cheese with the egg white until combined.
2. Spread the mixture into two small circles and season as desired.
3. Bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes until set and golden.
4. Top each with a runny egg, avocado, chives, hot sauce, or anything you like.

## Notes

Bake at 400°F for 20-25 minutes. Season the filling and add toppings to taste.
