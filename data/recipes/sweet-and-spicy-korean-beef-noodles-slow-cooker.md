## Ingredients

- 2lbs 96% beef
- 3 tbsp gochujang
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp sugar-free ketchup
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
- black pepper and salt to taste
- green onion whites (save greens for garnish)
- 5 packets Momofuku Sweet & Spicy noodles (425g total)
- 1 cup water
- 60g honey
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- a pinch of salt

### For the spicy mayo

- 60g light mayo
- 40g gochujang
- 20g sriracha
- 10g yuzu ponzu
- 10g sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Slow cook the beef with the gochujang, soy sauce, ketchup, garlic powder, rice wine vinegar, salt, pepper and green onion whites on low for 3-4 hours.
2. Mix the light mayo, gochujang, sriracha, yuzu ponzu and sesame oil for the spicy mayo.
3. Add the noodles, water, honey, sesame oil and salt to the slow cooker and cover for 10-15 minutes to finish the noodles.
4. Divide into 7 equal servings and garnish with green onion greens and spicy mayo.

## Notes

You can use any 425g noodles and a store-bought Korean BBQ marinade. Freeze the portions; microwave with 1 tablespoon of water for 3-4 minutes to reheat.
