## Ingredients

- 260g glutinous rice flour
- 180g unsalted butter
- 150g brown sugar
- 70g cocoa powder
- 4 tsp instant coffee powder
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 720ml milk (or plant-based milk)
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 180g chopped dark chocolate
- Flakey salt

## Instructions

1. Line two baking trays with baking paper and preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. Brown the butter in a saucepan over low-medium heat until fragrant, stir in the instant coffee and leave to cool.
3. Whisk the rice flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder and salt together in a large bowl.
4. Add the milk, beaten eggs and cooled coffee butter; whisk until smooth.
5. Pour into the lined trays, scatter the chopped chocolate evenly over the top, and bake for 45 minutes.
6. Cool completely, sprinkle with flaky salt and slice into squares.
