## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- black pepper
- lemon juice
- 30ml light soy sauce
- 30g cornflour
- 20g sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon garlic
- 1 teaspoon ginger
- 100ml light soy sauce
- lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
- 20g honey
- 10g sesame seeds
- 200g uncooked rice

## Instructions

1. Slice the chicken into strips and season with garlic powder, black pepper, lemon juice, soy sauce and cornflour.
2. Cook on a hot pan 10-15 minutes with the sesame oil until crispy (or oven/air fry 15 minutes at 220C), then set aside.
3. In the same pan over medium heat add garlic, ginger, soy sauce, lemon juice, rice vinegar and honey.
4. Return the chicken, stir until glazed, and finish with sesame seeds.
5. Boil the rice (200g uncooked yields about 630g cooked) and portion everything equally into containers.
