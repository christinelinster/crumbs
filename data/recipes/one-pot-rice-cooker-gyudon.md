## Ingredients

- 10 oz thinly sliced eye of round beef (shabushabu)
- 2/3 cups jasmine rice, uncooked/raw
- 1/3 cup beef bone broth
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 tsp dashi powder
- 1 yellow onion
- 1/2 tbsp ginger
- 2 eggs (boil for 6 min)
- 1 green onion

## Instructions

1. Slice the green onions thinly.
2. Rinse the rice.
3. Add the rice, beef, bone broth, soy sauce, mirin, dashi powder, yellow onion, and ginger to a rice cooker.
4. Cook for 30 minutes in the rice cooker.
5. Boil the eggs for 6 minutes, then move them to an ice bath for jammy yolks.
6. Bowl up half the gyudon, top with a boiled egg and half the green onions.
