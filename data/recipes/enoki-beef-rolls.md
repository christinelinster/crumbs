## Ingredients

- 1 lb thinly sliced beef (shabu shabu beef)
- 7 oz enoki mushrooms
- 1 white onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp oil (avocado oil preferred)
- 2 eggs, whisked
- chopped green onions and sesame seeds, to garnish

### For the sauce

- 1/3 cup water (sauce)
- 1/4 cup soy sauce (sauce)
- 1 tbsp sesame oil (sauce)
- 1 garlic clove, minced (sauce)
- dash of pepper (sauce)

## Instructions

1. Trim the bottom 1-2 inches off the enoki, rinse, and separate into small bundles.
2. Wrap each mushroom bundle tightly in a slice of beef; repeat until all are rolled.
3. Whisk the sauce ingredients together in a bowl and set aside.
4. Soften the sliced onion in hot oil for about 3 minutes.
5. Lay the beef rolls over the onions in a single layer, pour in the sauce, cover, bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer 3 minutes until the beef is cooked through.
6. Pour the whisked eggs over the rolls, cover, and cook 1 minute more.
7. Season with salt to taste and finish with green onions and sesame seeds.
