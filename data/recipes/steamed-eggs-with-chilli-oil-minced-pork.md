## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 2 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- 1/4 tsp ground white pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 120g minced pork
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp shaoxing wine
- 1 1/2 tbsp chilli oil
- 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tsp water (slurry)
- sesame oil and light soy sauce, to serve
- 1 spring onion, sliced

## Instructions

1. Gently whisk the eggs in a measuring jug and add double the volume of warm water (1:2 egg-to-water ratio).
2. Whisk in the seasonings and strain through a sieve for a silky texture.
3. Pour into a shallow heatproof bowl and cover with a heatproof plate.
4. Set an inverted bowl in a wok with water halfway up; bring to a boil, then rest the egg bowl on top and steam on medium-low for 15 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, fry the minced pork with its seasonings and cornstarch slurry until cooked and crispy.
6. Let the eggs sit 1 minute off the heat (a jiggly center means they are ready), then serve topped with sesame oil, soy sauce, and spring onion.

## Notes

Water temperature and volume affect the texture; the 15-minute steam time may vary by stove.
