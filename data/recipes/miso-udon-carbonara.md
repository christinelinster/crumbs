## Ingredients

- 200g udon
- 5g butter
- 15g pork belly slices
- 6g garlic
- 3 egg yolks
- 75g parmesan
- Green onions
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, whisk together the egg yolks, grated parmesan, and black pepper to form the carbonara sauce.
2. In a pan, melt the butter and sauté the pork belly slices with the minced garlic until the pork is golden and its fat has rendered.
3. Boil the udon noodles according to package directions, then drain.
4. Add the hot udon and pork belly to the bowl of sauce, tossing quickly off the heat so the residual heat emulsifies the sauce without scrambling the yolks.
5. Transfer to a serving bowl and top with an extra raw egg yolk, more parmesan, and chopped green onions.
