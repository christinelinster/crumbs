## Ingredients

- 1 large carrot
- 1 avocado
- 3 soft-boiled eggs
- Half red onion
- 1 Canned tuna
- 2 large tbsp of Greek yogurt (can be replaced with mayo)
- 1/2 tbsp of soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp of wasabi
- Half of lemon
- Sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Wrap the carrot in plastic wrap and microwave at 600W for 4-6 minutes until softened.
2. Soft-boil the eggs for 6 minutes in boiling water, then move them to ice water for 5-10 minutes and peel.
3. Slice the red onion thinly.
4. Whisk together the Greek yogurt, soy sauce, wasabi, lemon juice, and sesame seeds to make the dressing.
5. Add the avocado, cooked carrot, soft-boiled eggs, onion, and tuna to the dressing bowl.
6. Smash everything together and mix gently until combined.
7. Top with an extra soft-boiled egg, a sprinkle of pepper, and serve.
