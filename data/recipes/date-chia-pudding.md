## Ingredients

- 3 Medjool dates
- 2 tbsp hot water
- 2/3 cup vanilla or Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 3 tbsp chia seeds
- 1/3 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Soften the dates in the hot water, then blend or mash them into a paste.
2. Mix the date paste with the yogurt, peanut butter, chia seeds and milk.
3. Refrigerate for 4 hours or overnight before eating.
