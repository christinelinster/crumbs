## Ingredients

- 1-1.5 pounds seedless cucumbers (about 2 English cucumbers)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 1/2 teaspoons sugar
- 2 teaspoons sesame oil
- 3 teaspoons light soy sauce
- 1 1/2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2-4 cloves garlic (finely chopped)
- 1-2 teaspoons chili oil (optional)
- 2 teaspoons toasted sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro


## Instructions

1. Wash and dry the cucumbers. Mix the salt, sugar, sesame oil, light soy sauce, and rice vinegar until dissolved. Set aside.
2. Lay a large knife flat against each cucumber and gently smash until it splits into 4 long pieces. Cut into bite-size pieces at a 45-degree angle.
3. Toss the cucumber with the dressing, garlic, and chili oil. Garnish with sesame seeds and cilantro. To omit the chili oil, drizzle with 1 tablespoon heated oil instead.
