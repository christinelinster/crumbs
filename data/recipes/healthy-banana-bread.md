## Ingredients

- 3 large very ripe bananas
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 3/4 cup oat flour (210g)
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp mini chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Mash the bananas in a bowl, then add the eggs and vanilla and mix.
2. Add the oat flour, cinnamon, baking soda and salt (everything except the chocolate chips) and mix until smooth.
3. Pour into a lined or greased glass loaf pan.
4. Bake at 350F for 30-35 minutes.

## Notes

Weigh the 210g of flour instead of scooping it for better accuracy.
