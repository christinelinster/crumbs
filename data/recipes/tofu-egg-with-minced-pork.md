## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 150 g minced pork
- 300 g silken tofu
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1-2 tsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1/4 cup water

## Instructions

1. Marinate the minced pork with the oyster sauce, light soy sauce, sugar, and salt for 20 minutes; mix the sauce ingredients separately.
2. Brown the pork in 1 tbsp oil over medium heat, breaking it up with a spatula, about 2 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, fry a minute, then stir in the prepared sauce, cook a minute more, and set aside.
4. Beat the eggs in a separate bowl.
5. Add another 1 tbsp oil to the same pan, arrange the tofu cubes in the center, and cook 1-2 minutes over medium-high heat before pouring in the eggs.
6. When the eggs are runny but starting to set, lower the heat, tilt the pan, and push the set edges toward the center.
7. Once the eggs are cooked, spoon the pork over the top and garnish with green onions or chili.
