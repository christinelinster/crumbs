## Ingredients

- 25oz chicken breast, thin sliced and diced
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp avocado oil (or olive oil)
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 300g mixed frozen veggies of choice
- 200g egg whites
- 3 eggs
- 700g day-old cooked rice (300g dry weight)
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp sesame oil

## Instructions

1. Season and cook the sliced chicken breast.
2. Scramble the egg whites and eggs separately and set aside.
3. Cook the frozen vegetables and the day-old rice in sesame oil with soy sauce and sriracha.
4. Combine the chicken, eggs, and rice mixture and toss until heated through.
