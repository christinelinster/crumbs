## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice + the regular amount of water you normally use for your rice
- 1 lb chicken thigh, diced
- 1/4 yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon hondashi
- 1 teaspoon mirin
- Pinch of sugar
- 2-3 eggs
- Green onions and sesame seeds, to garnish

## Instructions

1. Wash the rice and start it in the rice cooker with water as usual.
2. Mix the soy sauce, hondashi, mirin, and sugar in a bowl; toss the diced chicken and sliced onion through it.
3. Spoon the chicken-onion mixture (with all its sauce) over the raw rice in the cooker.
4. Cook the rice normally; meanwhile beat the eggs with chopsticks or a fork for about 20 seconds.
5. As soon as the rice finishes, pour the beaten egg over the chicken and rice, close the lid immediately, and run the rice-cook setting once more for 2 minutes.
6. Serve garnished with green onions and sesame seeds.
