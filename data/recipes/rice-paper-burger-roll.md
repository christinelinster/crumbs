## Ingredients

- 3 sheets rice paper
- 150g extra lean ground beef (cooked)
- diced onions
- diced pickles
- sliced tomatoes
- salt and pepper
- garlic powder
- sesame seeds
- 1 egg

### For the burger sauce

- 20g light or fat free mayonnaise
- 10g yellow mustard
- 10ml pickle juice
- 10g sugar free ketchup
- 2 tbsp diced pickles
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- pinch of salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Cook the extra lean ground beef on high heat with salt, pepper, and garlic powder until the edges turn crispy.
2. Mix all the burger sauce ingredients together.
3. Whisk the egg on a plate; coat each rice paper sheet one by one and lay them horizontally with slight overlap.
4. Place the filling on the rice paper and roll with the ends tucked in; fold into a swirl shape.
5. Coat in egg wash and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
6. Bake or air fry at 350F for 15-17 minutes and enjoy.
