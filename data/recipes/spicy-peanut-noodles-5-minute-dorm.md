## Ingredients

- 120g favorite noodles
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tbsp Angry Grandma chili oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 0.5 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- 2-3 tbsp hot water
- Angry Grandma chili oil, chives, furikake or sesame seeds for garnish
- egg yolk (optional)

## Instructions

1. Cook the noodles according to package instructions.
2. Whisk all the sauce ingredients in a bowl, adjusting with hot water.
3. Drain the noodles and add them straight into the sauce; toss well.
4. Garnish with chili oil, chives, furikake or sesame seeds, and the optional egg yolk.

## Notes

The egg yolk is optional; adjust the hot water to the desired consistency.
