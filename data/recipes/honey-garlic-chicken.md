## Ingredients

- chicken (amount not specified)
- seasonings of your choice: onion powder, paprika, salt, pepper, garlic powder

### For the honey garlic glaze

- honey
- garlic
- olive oil

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with the seasonings and drizzle with a little olive oil, then massage the seasonings in.
2. Arrange evenly in the air fryer and bake 18-20 minutes at 385-400F depending on desired crispness.
3. A couple of minutes before the chicken is done, heat honey in a pan with about 1-2 tbsp of garlic until sizzling.
4. Add the chicken to the honey-garlic mixture and toss until evenly coated.
