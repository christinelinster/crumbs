## Ingredients

- premium Thai wagyu beef (3 cuts)
- 3 tbsp shoyu
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- pepper
- cooking oil, garlic, onion
- Japanese rice
- corn, green onion, salted butter

## Instructions

1. Stir the shoyu, mirin, oyster sauce, brown sugar and pepper into a sauce.
2. Heat oil in the pan and stir-fry the garlic and onion until fragrant.
3. Place the Japanese rice in the center of the pan with the beef arranged around it, then add corn, green onion and salted butter on top.
4. Pour over the sauce, season with extra pepper to taste, and stir-fry everything together before serving.
