## Ingredients

- 40g oats
- 10g chia seeds
- 1 egg
- pinch of salt
- 120ml water
- wrap fillings of choice (lettuce, scrambled egg, tomato, cream cheese or Greek yogurt, mayonnaise)

## Instructions

1. Blend the oats, chia, egg, salt, and water until completely smooth, then rest the batter 5 minutes.
2. Cook in a pan like a thin tortilla.
3. Fill with whatever toppings you like and roll up.
