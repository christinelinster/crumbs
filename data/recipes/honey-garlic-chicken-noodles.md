## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast
- 30ml light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 15g sriracha
- 1 tbsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 600g cooked noodles
- spring onion (quantity not specified)

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp garlic
- 1 teaspoon ginger
- 70ml light soy sauce
- 15g sriracha
- 15g honey
- 1 tbsp hoisin sauce

## Instructions

1. Cut the chicken into pieces and season with soy sauce, garlic powder, sriracha, black pepper and sesame oil.
2. Cook on high heat for 10 minutes (or oven/air fry 15 minutes at 220C), then set aside.
3. Over medium heat add garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sriracha, honey and hoisin sauce.
4. Add the cooked noodles and chicken, stir together and simmer 5 minutes.
5. Finish with spring onion and divide into 5 meal prep containers.
