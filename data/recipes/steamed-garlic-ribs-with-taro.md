## Ingredients

- 1 pound pork ribs (cut into 1-2 inch/3-5 cm pieces; your butcher can do this for you)
- 1 tablespoon clear rice wine
- 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 teaspoons oyster sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon sesame oil
- 1/8 teaspoon ground white pepper
- 1 1/2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon water
- 2 tablespoons oil (any neutral oil)
- 3 tablespoons garlic (chopped, from about 9 cloves)
- 1 1/3 cups taro (diced into 1/2-inch cubes)
- 1 tablespoon scallion (chopped, to garnish; optional)


## Instructions

1. Mix the ribs with the rice wine, light soy sauce, sugar, salt, oyster sauce, sesame oil, and white pepper. Cover and marinate overnight for best tenderness, or at least 1 hour (2 hours for meatier ribs).
2. Mix the cornstarch and water into the ribs until no liquid remains at the bottom of the bowl.
3. Heat the oil and garlic in a saucepan over low heat for 4-5 minutes, until fragrant and translucent but not browned.
4. Arrange the taro in a single layer in a shallow heatproof bowl. Place the ribs on top, then spoon the garlic and oil over them.
5. Bring water to a simmer in a steamer or wok with a rack. Add the bowl, cover, and steam for 20-25 minutes, until the ribs are opaque and the taro is tender.
6. Garnish with scallion, if desired. Serve over rice with a stir-fried vegetable.
