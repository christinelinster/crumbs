## Ingredients

- 1 serving of udon noodles
- 150g chicken breast/thighs
- 1/2 tbsp miso paste
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp green onion, sliced
- 1/2 cup milk (sub water)
- 1 cup water
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 egg
- soy sauce (to taste)
- dumplings (optional)
- enoki mushrooms (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat; briefly saute the green onion and garlic for about a minute, then stir in the miso paste.
2. Add the chicken and stir-fry 5-7 minutes until cooked through, then pour in the water and milk, raise to medium-high heat, and simmer 3 minutes.
3. Once boiling, add the udon plus dumplings, enoki mushrooms, or other extras; season with soy sauce, crack in the egg, drizzle with sesame oil, cook about a minute, then finish with more green onion.
