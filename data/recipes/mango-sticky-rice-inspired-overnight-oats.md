## Ingredients

- 1 mango, cut into chunks
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 2 tbsp chia seeds (optional)
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp honey
- a sprinkle of salt

## Instructions

1. Combine the oats, chia seeds, coconut milk, water, Greek yogurt, vanilla, honey and salt in a container.
2. Fold in the mango chunks.
3. Refrigerate overnight and serve cold in the morning.
