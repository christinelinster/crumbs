## Ingredients

- 200g skinless chicken thigh
- 100g firm tofu
- any herbal chicken soup block
- 100g napa cabbage
- 80g enoki mushrooms

## Instructions

1. Pack all the ingredients into a freezer bag for one pot; freeze.
2. To serve: add water and boil.
