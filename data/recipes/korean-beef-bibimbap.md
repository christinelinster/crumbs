## Ingredients

- 2lbs ground beef 95/5 lean, raw
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1.5 tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/4 cup carrot
- 1/4 cup shiitake mushrooms
- 1/4 cup bean sprouts
- 2 cup spinach
- 8 eggs
- 1 second spray oil
- 3 cup cooked rice

### For the bibimbap sauce

- 3/4 cup gochujang
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 3 tbsp sesame oil
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- 6 tbsp rice vinegar

## Instructions

1. Slice the mushrooms and carrots thinly.
2. Cook the mushrooms, carrots, bean sprouts, and spinach in a pan coated with spray oil over medium-low heat with the lid on, adding a few splashes of water so they steam; remove from the pan when done.
3. Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in a bowl.
4. Brown the raw ground beef in the same pan.
5. Season the beef with salt, pepper, soy sauce, and sesame oil; cook until done, then remove.
6. Fry the eggs sunny-side up with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Assemble each meal prep: one fried egg, 1/8 of the cooked rice, steamed vegetables, beef, and sauce on top.
8. To reheat, microwave with a damp paper towel, then mix everything together.
