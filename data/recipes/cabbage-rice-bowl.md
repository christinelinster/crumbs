## Ingredients

- 200g cabbage
- 1/2 onion
- 1/2 green onion
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/3 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Sesame seeds for garnish

## Instructions

1. Slice the green onion, onion, and cabbage thinly.
2. Warm olive oil in a pan over low heat and saute the green onion and garlic until fragrant.
3. Add the onion, cabbage, soy sauce, sugar, and oyster sauce; stir-fry over high heat.
4. Keep cooking until the vegetables release some moisture and turn slightly saucy, then crack an egg in the center and cook to your liking.
5. Serve the cabbage stir-fry over white rice, brown rice, or tofu as you prefer.
