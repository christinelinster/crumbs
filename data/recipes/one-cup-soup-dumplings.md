## Ingredients

- 12oz ground pork, raw
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 green onion, medium
- 1/2 tbsp chinese cooking wine
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/4 cup chicken bone broth
- 8 sheets wonton wrapper
- 1/4 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 green onion (topping)

## Instructions

1. Mix the ground pork with the garlic, sesame oil, soy sauce, and black pepper.
2. In each small ceramic pot, layer pork mix, a wonton wrapper, more pork mix, and another wrapper.
3. Pour the bone broth into each pot.
4. Steam all the pots 11-13 minutes until the pork is cooked through.
5. Top with sesame seeds and diced green onion.

## Notes

The instructions use sesame oil, but the ingredient list only includes sesame seeds and gives no sesame oil amount.
