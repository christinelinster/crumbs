## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup fresh blueberries
- 1-2 tbsp honey
- 3/4 cup dairy-free milk
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- a pinch of sea salt
- double shot espresso
- ice

## Instructions

1. Blend the blueberries and honey with an immersion blender until broken down and slightly fluffy.
2. Blend the milk, peanut butter and sea salt until smooth and creamy.
3. Line a glass with peanut butter and add ice.
4. Strain the blueberry mixture into the bottom of the glass, then pour in the peanut butter milk.
5. Brew a double shot of espresso and pour over the top.
6. Stir or leave layered and enjoy.
