## Ingredients

- 9-10 English muffins of choice
- 9-10 slices of cheese of choice

### For the eggs

- 12 eggs
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 1/4 tsp pepper

### For the sausage

- 2 lb ground chicken
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp poultry seasoning
- 1/2 tsp dried sage
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp sea salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- red pepper flakes, to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375F and line a 9x13-inch baking pan with parchment paper.
2. Blend the egg ingredients until smooth; pour into the pan and bake 17-20 minutes until the eggs are cooked through; set aside.
3. Mix all the sausage ingredients until well combined and divide into 9-10 equal portions.
4. Heat a large pan over medium heat; add the patties, pressing them down as they shrink, and cook 3-4 minutes until browned; flip and cook 2-3 more minutes until cooked through.
5. Cut the egg bake into circles or squares, saving any scraps to add to sandwiches.
6. Slice the English muffins; layer on an egg patty, a sausage patty, and a slice of cheese; wrap each sandwich in parchment or foil and freeze.
7. To reheat: thaw in the refrigerator overnight; microwave in a damp paper towel about 2 minutes, or bake in a 350F oven until heated through.
