## Ingredients

- 220g fettuccine
- 100g diced bacon
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 cloves minced garlic
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- pinch of red chili flakes
- 50g grated parmesan cheese
- pinch of salt and pepper

## Instructions

1. Boil the pasta in generously salted water; reserve 1/4 cup pasta water, then drain.
2. Fry the bacon until crispy, move it to a paper towel, and keep 1 tablespoon of the bacon grease.
3. In the same pan melt the butter, saute the garlic about 30 seconds until fragrant, then whisk in the heavy cream and simmer.
4. Whisk in the parmesan until fully melted.
5. Return the pasta and bacon to the pan with the reserved pasta water, toss, garnish with more parmesan and chives, and serve.
