## Ingredients

- 7 persian cucumbers
- 1/2 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1.5 tbsp gochugaru
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumbers into long strips with a peeler.
2. Toss the strips with salt, let them sit 20 minutes, then drain, rinse, and pat dry.
3. Whisk together the vinegar, garlic, sesame oil, gochugaru, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame seeds, and salt to taste.
4. Fold the cucumber strips into the marinade until coated, then pack into an airtight container to keep.
