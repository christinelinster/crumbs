## Ingredients

- 100g frozen strawberries
- 150g greek yogurt
- 75g milk
- 75g water
- 40-50g honey (depending how sweet you like it)

## Instructions

1. Add the frozen strawberries, Greek yogurt, milk, water, and honey to a blender.
2. Blend until completely smooth and pink.
3. Taste and add a little more honey if you want it sweeter, then blend again.
4. Pour into a glass or bottle and serve immediately.
