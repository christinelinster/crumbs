## Ingredients

- 3 bananas
- 1/2 cup peanut butter
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 1/2 cups oats (can use half flour)
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1 tsp vanilla
- chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Combine the bananas, peanut butter, maple syrup, egg and milk in one bowl.
2. Add the oats, baking powder, cinnamon, vanilla and chocolate chips and mix until just combined.
3. Pour into a lined or greased pan and bake at 350F for 30-35 minutes.
