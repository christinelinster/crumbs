## Ingredients

- 2 medium ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/2 cup peanut butter or tahini
- 2-4 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 cup oat flour
- 1/4 cup seed blend (ground flax, hemp, pumpkin seeds)
- 1/4 cup collagen peptides
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cup old fashioned rolled oats
- 1/2 cup dark chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F and line a large baking sheet with parchment.
2. Stir the mashed bananas, nut butter, maple syrup, egg, and vanilla until smooth.
3. Fold in the flour, oats, seed blend, collagen, baking soda, and salt until just combined, then stir in the chocolate chips.
4. Let the dough rest for 5-10 minutes.
5. Scoop roughly 3-tablespoon mounds of dough onto the prepared sheets.
6. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until set but still soft.
