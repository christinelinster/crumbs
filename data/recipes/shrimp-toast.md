## Ingredients

- 1 lb shrimp, minced
- 1 egg, whites only
- 1 tbsp corn starch
- Lots of scallions
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp black sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. In a bowl, mix the minced shrimp with the egg whites, corn starch, scallions, fish sauce, sugar, and sesame oil until well combined and slightly sticky.
2. Spread a thick layer of the shrimp mixture onto slices of white bread.
3. Cook using either method: air-fry at 400°F for about 6 minutes per side, or deep-fry in 350°F oil for about 2 minutes per side, until golden brown and crisp.
4. Drain briefly if deep-fried, then sprinkle with black sesame seeds and serve hot.
