## Ingredients

- Sweet potatoes

## Instructions

1. Trim the tips off both ends of each sweet potato.
2. Bake at 375 F on the top rack.
3. Give them a 2-minute ice bath.
4. Return to the oven at 425 F for 30 minutes.
