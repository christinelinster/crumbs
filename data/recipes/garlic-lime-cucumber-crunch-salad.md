## Ingredients

- 4 or 5 Persian cucumbers (or 3 Japanese cucumbers), thinly sliced
- 8oz finely chopped broccoli
- 1 avocado, cubed
- 1 large handful chopped mint and chives
- 2 tbsp roasted walnuts or pistachios
- 2 tbsp mayo
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- zest of 1 lime
- juice of 2 limes
- 1-2 minced garlic cloves
- generous pinch of salt
- optional: feta, extra herbs, extra nuts, sumac

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumbers thin so they absorb the dressing well.
2. Add the cucumbers to a large bowl or jar with the chopped broccoli, cubed avocado, and the mint and chives.
3. Scatter over the nuts.
4. Whisk the mayo, yogurt, Dijon, lime zest and juice, garlic, and salt into a creamy dressing.
5. Pour over the vegetables and shake or toss to coat, then finish with any of the optional toppings.
