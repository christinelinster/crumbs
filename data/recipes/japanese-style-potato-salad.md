## Ingredients

- 2 potatoes
- 4 soft-boiled eggs (one is topping)
- Half cucumber
- Bacon
- 2 tbsp of Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp of mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp of mustard
- 2 tbsp of milk
- Black pepper

## Instructions

1. Wrap the potatoes in plastic wrap and microwave at 600W for 4-6 minutes; peel the skins using kitchen paper.
2. Soft-boil the eggs for 6 minutes in boiling water, then rest them in ice water for 5-10 minutes and peel.
3. Slice the cucumber, salt it lightly, let it sit about 5 minutes, then squeeze out the excess water.
4. Chop the bacon and cook in a pan until crisp.
5. Stir together the yogurt, mayonnaise, mustard, milk, and black pepper for the dressing.
6. Add the eggs, potatoes, cucumber, and bacon to the dressing bowl.
7. Smash the potatoes and eggs, then fold everything together gently.
8. Finish with a soft-boiled egg on top and a grind of black pepper.
