## Ingredients

- 2 english cucumbers
- 2 scallions
- 1 clove garlic sliced thin
- 1.5 tbsp sesame oil
- 3 tbsp light soy sauce
- 3 tbsp Honey
- 4 tbsp rice vinegar
- 3 tbsp chili oil
- 2 tbsp sesame seeds

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and toss well so the cucumbers are evenly coated.
