## Ingredients

- 10 rice paper sheets
- 2 eggs
- 1 tbsp nut milk
- 1 tbsp coconut sugar
- 1 tsp melted coconut oil
- 1.5 bananas (sliced in halves then halved again)
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 2 cubes chocolate
- 1 tbsp extra coconut sugar for dusting

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs, nut milk and coconut sugar together; melt the coconut oil separately and let it cool slightly.
2. Coat the banana pieces in cinnamon.
3. Stir the coconut oil into the egg mixture (if it solidifies while working, warm it very gently for 10 seconds).
4. Dip each rice paper sheet into the egg mixture, letting excess drip off, and lay it on a chopping board; repeat so each pocket has two stacked sheets.
5. Place banana pieces in the centre, add chocolate and a sprinkle of coconut sugar, then fold into a pocket.
6. Air fry at 180°C for 10 minutes and serve.
