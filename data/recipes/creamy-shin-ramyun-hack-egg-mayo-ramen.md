## Ingredients

- 1 pack Shin Ramyun
- 1 egg
- about 1 tbsp Kewpie mayo
- 1 packet Shin Ramyun soup seasoning
- 1/2 the veggie flakes packet (optional)
- green onions, finely chopped (for topping)
- chili oil (for topping)
- water (for cooking noodles)

## Instructions

1. Boil 2-3 cups of water and cook the noodles about 4-5 minutes; add the veggie flakes if you like them.
2. Whisk the egg, mayo and about two-thirds of the seasoning packet in a large bowl until smooth and creamy.
3. Ladle about a cup of the hot noodle water into the bowl, stirring quickly so the egg tempers instead of scrambling.
4. Add the cooked noodles and toss everything together.
5. Top with chopped green onions and a generous drizzle of chili oil.

## Notes

Use about two-thirds of the seasoning packet to avoid over-salting.
