## Ingredients

- 2-3 tablespoons chia seeds
- 4 teaspoons matcha powder (sifted)
- Milk of choice (coconut or almond works great)
- 1 tablespoon sweetener (honey or maple syrup)
- Greek yogurt for layering
- Fresh mango, diced into small cubes
- Topping: pumpkin seeds

## Instructions

1. Add the chia seeds to a bowl and sift the matcha powder evenly over them.
2. Pour in the milk and add the honey or maple syrup.
3. Whisk thoroughly until smooth and evenly green, with no matcha clumps.
4. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight, until set.
5. Spoon the set pudding into a glass, alternating layers with Greek yogurt and diced mango to build a parfait.
6. Top with pumpkin seeds and serve chilled.
