## Ingredients

- ~3 lb chuck roast
- Seasonings: Salt, pepper & garlic powder
- ~1-2 tbsp of ghee
- 2 bay leaves
- ~1.5-2 cups beef broth
- ~2-4 chipotles in adobo sauce
- ~1-2 tbsp of adobo sauce
- ~2 tbsp of minced garlic
- ~2 tbsp of apple cidar vinegar
- Juice of a lime
- ~ 2 tbsp cumin
- ~ 2 tbsp mexican oregano
- ~ 1 tbsp onion powder
- ~ 1 tbsp garlic powder
- ~ 1/2 tbsp salt
- ~ 1/2 tbsp pepper

## Instructions

1. Blend all the braising liquid ingredients together and set aside.
2. Pat the chuck roast dry, season generously on all sides, and sear in ghee for 3-4 minutes per side over medium-high heat.
3. Spread a layer of the liquid in the crockpot, add the roast, pour in the rest, and tuck in the bay leaves.
4. Cook on high for 4-6 hours, until fork-tender and starting to fall apart.
5. Rest a few minutes, shred the beef, and spoon some of the cooking broth over it.
6. Serve in tacos, bowls, or burritos.
