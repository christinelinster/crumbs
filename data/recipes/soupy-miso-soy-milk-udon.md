## Ingredients

- cooking spray (3 sec)
- 8 oz lean ground meat
- 1/4 cup onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- 1/2 tsp dashi powder
- 1 tsp monkfruit sugar
- 1 cup soy milk (1.5 cup if you want it soupier)
- 1 serving udon (fresh or frozen)
- marinated egg
- scallions
- seaweed

## Instructions

1. Spray a pan over medium-high heat and cook the ground meat.
2. Add the garlic and onion and cook until soft and translucent.
3. Work in the miso paste, then add the mirin to deglaze, and stir in the dashi powder and monkfruit sugar.
4. Reserve the extra base for future recipes.
5. Pour in the soy milk and warm through over medium heat.
6. Add the udon directly to the broth and cook, stirring, letting the starch naturally thicken the soup.
7. Garnish with the egg, scallions, and seaweed; serve hot.
