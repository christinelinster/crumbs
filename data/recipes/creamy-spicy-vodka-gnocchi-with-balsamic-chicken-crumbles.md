## Ingredients

- 1 cup of low fat cottage cheese
- 1 cup of your favorite pasta sauce (creator used Carbone spicy vodka sauce)
- 1/2 cup (2 oz) of grated parmesan
- 1 pound of ground chicken breast or turkey
- 2 tablespoons each of chopped Calabrian chili peppers, balsamic vinegar, honey, and water
- 4 cubes of frozen crushed garlic (or 4 cloves of fresh garlic)
- 1 pound of potato gnocchi
- Freshly chopped basil
- Black pepper
- Low fat ricotta (for garnish)
- Drizzle of olive oil (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, pasta sauce, and parmesan until completely smooth; set aside.
3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with cooking spray or a little olive oil.
4. Cook the ground chicken on one side until golden, then break it up and cook through.
5. Stir in the Calabrian chilies, balsamic, honey, water, and garlic; simmer until the sauce thickens, then lower the heat.
6. Cook the gnocchi 2-3 minutes until they float; reserve about a cup of the cooking water before draining.
7. Toss the gnocchi with the blended sauce and about three-quarters of the chicken over low heat, adding reserved pasta water as needed to make it silky.
8. Season with salt, plate, and top with the remaining chicken, basil, pepper, ricotta, and olive oil.
