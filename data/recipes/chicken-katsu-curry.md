## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- black pepper
- chopped garlic
- 30g chopped onions
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp cumin
- 300ml chicken stock
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- ~10g honey (a drizzle)
- 200g uncooked washed white rice (makes roughly 630g cooked)

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken with soy sauce, cumin, chilli powder, turmeric and black pepper, then oven or air fry at 220C for 15 minutes until golden brown.
2. On medium heat, cook the chopped garlic, onions, ginger and cumin in a pan.
3. Add the chicken stock, soy sauce and honey, simmer for 5 minutes, then blend until thick.
4. Boil the rice (200g uncooked yields about 630g cooked).
5. Divide into 5 meal prep containers with roughly 125g of cooked rice each.

## Notes

Portion about 125g cooked rice into each container.
