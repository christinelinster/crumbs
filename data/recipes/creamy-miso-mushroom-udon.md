## Ingredients

- 1 serving of udon noodles
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp garlic, minced
- 1/2 medium onion, sliced
- 100-150g mushrooms of your choice
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or milk
- 1 tbsp miso paste
- black pepper and salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Boil the frozen udon for about 30 seconds, then strain and set aside.
2. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat and soften the onions for about 2 minutes.
3. Add the mushrooms and cook about 3 minutes until browned.
4. Stir in the garlic for about a minute, then pour in the cream or milk; raise to medium-high heat and simmer for 3 minutes.
5. Season with salt and black pepper.
6. Once the sauce starts thickening, add the cooked udon and toss for about a minute; serve immediately.
