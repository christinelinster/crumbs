## Ingredients

- 650g chicken thighs
- 4-5 slices of ginger
- 4-5 cloves garlic
- 2-3 chinese dried chilli (optional)
- 10ml sesame oil
- 20ml chinese cooking wine
- 10ml dark soy sauce + 20ml light soy sauce combined
- 1 tsp rock sugar
- small bunch of thai basil leaves

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a wok over medium heat and infuse the ginger, garlic, and dried chili for a couple of minutes.
2. Raise the heat to high and sear the chicken in a single layer until golden on both sides.
3. Pour in the cooking wine, soy sauces, and rock sugar; stir, cover, and simmer on medium about 15 minutes until the chicken cooks through.
4. Uncover, turn up the heat, and reduce the sauce for a few minutes until the chicken takes on a rich color.
5. Stir in the Thai basil for a final minute until wilted.
