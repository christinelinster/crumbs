## Ingredients

### Dry Ingredients

- 1/2 cup all purpose flour, spooned and leveled
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

### Wet Ingredients

- 3/4 cup low fat cottage cheese (2%)
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil, or melted butter
- 1/2 tbsp vanilla extract
- 1 1/2 Tbsp maple syrup
- 2/3 cup blueberries, washed and dried

### For cooking

- salted butter

## Instructions

1. To a large mixing bowl, add the flour, baking powder, and salt. Whisk until combined and set aside.
2. To a small blender, add all wet ingredients except the blueberries. Blend until completely smooth, about 20 seconds.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Use a wooden spoon or spatula to gently stir until a batter forms. Gently fold in the blueberries. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while the pan heats (it needs this time to thicken), but no longer than 10 minutes.
4. Heat a non-stick pan to slightly below medium and coat with a thin layer of salted butter. If the pan is too hot, the outsides can burn before the insides cook through.
5. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter per pancake into the pan, leaving space between pancakes. Cook undisturbed for 3-4 minutes, until the tops have lots of bubbles and the bottoms are golden brown. Flip and cook for a few more minutes, or until cooked through.
6. Repeat with the remaining batter, adding butter to the pan as needed. Serve immediately with maple syrup, berries, and other toppings as desired.

## Notes

If your cottage cheese is particularly salty, reduce the salt by half.
