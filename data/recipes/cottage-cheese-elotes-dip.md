## Ingredients

- 1 can Del Monte street corn (drained)
- 1 cup boneless skinless shredded chicken breast
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 jalapeno
- 2 tbsp crema
- 1 16oz tub 2% @daisy cottage cheese
- 3 tbsp Chipotle Tabasco
- 2 tbsp Tajin
- 1/3 cup queso Fresco cheese
- Juice of 1 lime

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth and add it to a mixing bowl.
2. Shred the chicken breast and chop the cilantro and jalapeno; add them to the bowl.
3. Add all remaining ingredients and stir very thoroughly to combine.
4. Refrigerate for 2 hours so the flavors can meld before serving.
5. Serve with chips, on meat or tacos, or eat straight from the bowl.
