## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast, diced
- 1 tbsp garlic
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- black pepper
- 20g sriracha
- 30ml light soy sauce
- 15g sesame oil
- 1 tbsp garlic
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 30g peanut butter
- 50ml light soy sauce
- 100ml water
- 600g cooked egg noodles
- spring onion

## Instructions

1. Season the diced chicken with garlic, chilli powder, black pepper, sriracha and soy sauce, then cook on high heat in a pan for 10 minutes.
2. Set the chicken aside; add the sesame oil, garlic, chilli flakes and sesame seeds to the pan.
3. Stir in the peanut butter, soy sauce and water.
4. Add the cooked noodles and the chicken back in and toss everything together.
5. Finish with spring onion and divide into 5 meal prep containers.
