## Ingredients

- 9 cucumbers
- 1 tbsp salt
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp oil
- half a bulb of garlic, minced
- 1 shallot, sliced
- 3 stalks of coriander/cilantro, chopped
- 2 tbsp chili crisp
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- vinegar (recommended, not used here)

## Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl and toss until the cucumbers are evenly coated.
2. Adjust seasoning to taste, and refrigerate any leftovers.

## Notes

Season to taste.
