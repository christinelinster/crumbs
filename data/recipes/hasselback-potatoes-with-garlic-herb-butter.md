## Ingredients

- gold potatoes, scrubbed clean (number not specified)
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, softened
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- herbs of choice (chives and parsley used)
- salt and pepper to taste
- olive oil for drizzling

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450F.
2. Mix the softened butter with the garlic, herbs, salt and pepper, roll into a log in plastic wrap and chill at least 30 minutes.
3. Set each potato between two chopsticks and slice thinly without cutting all the way through.
4. Drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and bake 30 minutes or until the slices start to separate.
5. Tuck thin slices of the compound butter between the potato slices and bake another 30 minutes (longer for large potatoes).
6. Serve topped with another slice of compound butter, sour cream and chives.

## Notes

Check the potatoes during the second bake; timing varies with their size.
