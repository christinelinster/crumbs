## Ingredients

- 8-10 tomatoes
- 1 large onion
- 2 bell peppers
- 3 bulbs of garlic
- oil
- salt
- black pepper
- 1 cup chicken stock (or stock of choice)
- fresh basil
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 4 cans of tuna
- 1/2 medium shallot, finely diced
- 2 tbsp finely diced banana peppers
- 2 celery sticks, finely diced
- 1 tbsp chopped dill
- 1 tbsp chopped parsley
- 2-3 small pickles, finely diced
- 1/3 cup mayo
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1-2 tbsp hot sauce
- 2 tsp lemon juice
- sourdough bread
- butter
- cheeses (smoked cheddar, Havarti, and Parmesan)

## Instructions

1. Chop the tomatoes, onion, and bell peppers; spread on a baking tray with the garlic bulbs (tops cut off); coat in oil and season generously with salt.
2. Bake at 375F for 20-30 minutes until softened with some char.
3. Squeeze the roasted garlic from the bulbs; add everything to a pot with the stock, basil, and black pepper; blend until smooth, then stir in the heavy cream; taste and adjust seasoning, adding a pinch of sugar if too acidic.
4. Combine the tuna, shallot, banana peppers, celery, dill, parsley, pickles, mayo, Dijon, hot sauce, lemon juice, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
5. Build the sandwich: layer smoked cheddar, tuna mixture, Havarti, and Parmesan between sourdough slices.
6. Butter a pan over medium-low heat; cook the sandwich low and slow with a lid on so the cheese melts before the bread darkens; flip and toast the other side until golden.
7. Serve the sandwich with the tomato soup (and potato chips for the full experience).
