## Ingredients

- 1 pack pasta of choice
- 1-2 tbsp butter
- Bacon strip or diced chicken breast
- 1-2 tbsp miso paste
- 1 cup sliced button or swiss brown mushrooms
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup pasta water or as needed
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1 chopped spring onion
- Grated parmesan

## Instructions

1. Cook the pasta in well-salted boiling water until al dente; reserve at least 1/2 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and add the bacon; cook until crisp.
3. Add the sliced mushrooms to the pan and cook until softened and golden.
4. Stir in the miso paste, then pour in the heavy cream with a splash of pasta water and stir until the sauce is smooth and lightly thickened.
5. Add the cooked pasta to the pan and toss until every piece is evenly coated, loosening with more pasta water as needed.
6. Season with salt and pepper, then finish with chopped spring onion and grated parmesan.
