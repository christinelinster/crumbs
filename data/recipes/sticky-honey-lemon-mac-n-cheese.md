## Ingredients

- 600g raw chicken breast, diced
- 1 tbsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp oregano
- lemon juice
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 70ml light soy sauce
- 15g honey
- lemon juice
- parsley
- 500g cooked macaroni pasta

### For the cheese sauce

- 25g light butter
- 80ml hot sauce
- 60g light low-fat cream cheese
- 20g cheddar

## Instructions

1. Season the diced chicken with garlic powder, oregano, lemon juice, cumin and olive oil, then cook on high heat in a pan for 10 minutes.
2. Add the soy sauce, honey, lemon juice and parsley, then let it simmer for 5 minutes.
3. In a separate pan on low heat, melt the butter and stir in the hot sauce, cream cheese and cheddar until combined.
4. Add the cooked macaroni and stir until the sauce is creamy.
5. Divide into 5 equal meal prep containers.
