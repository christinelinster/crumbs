## Ingredients

- 1 strawberry protein shake (350-400ml)
- 20-25g monk fruit sweetener
- A pinch of salt
- 1g (1/4 tsp) xanthan gum
- Mix-ins: 1 heaping tbsp strawberry jam
- 15g chocolate chips + 2g coconut oil

## Instructions

1. Blend all base ingredients until smooth, then freeze for at least 16 hours.
2. Run the frozen pint under hot water for at least 60 seconds.
3. Spin on 'Lite Ice Cream', respinning (twice if needed) until creamy.
4. Melt the chocolate chips with the coconut oil in the microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring between.
5. Make a hole down the centre, add the jam and melted chocolate, and run the 'Mix-in' cycle.

## Notes

Requires a Ninja Creami.
