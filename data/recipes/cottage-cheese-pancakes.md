## Ingredients

- 1 tub cottage cheese (16oz)
- 4 eggs
- 2 tbs honey (or sugar)
- 1 tbs baking powder
- 1 c flour
- 1/4 c butter/oil
- 1 tsp vanilla (optional)

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese until smooth.
2. Add the eggs, honey, baking powder, flour, butter or oil, and vanilla (if using) to the blended cottage cheese and mix into a smooth batter.
3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and grease lightly.
4. Pour portions of batter into the pan to form pancakes.
5. Cook until the edges look set and the bottom is golden, then flip and cook the second side until golden.
6. Stack the pancakes and top with sliced banana and a dollop of whipped honey butter or your favorite creamy topping.
