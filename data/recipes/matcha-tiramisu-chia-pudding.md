## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1/3 cup chia seeds
- 2 tsp matcha
- 1/2 tbsp maple syrup
- Greek yogurt

## Instructions

1. Whisk the coconut milk, chia seeds, matcha and maple syrup until smooth and creamy in a mason jar or bowl.
2. Refrigerate overnight or at least a few hours until set.
3. Layer the chia pudding and Greek yogurt alternately in a tall glass, finishing with yogurt and a dusting of matcha.
4. Serve immediately or keep in the fridge up to 3 days.
