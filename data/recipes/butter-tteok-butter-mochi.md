## Ingredients

- 160g glutinous rice flour
- 60g sugar
- 30g unsalted butter
- 200ml milk
- 1 egg
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. Combine the glutinous rice flour, sugar and salt with the milk, egg, melted butter and vanilla, mixing into a smooth batter.
2. Bake at 180-185°C for 30 minutes until set and crisp on the outside.

## Notes

The baking dish size affects the mochi's thickness and baking time.
