## Ingredients

- 2 lbs skin on pork belly
- 10 small shallots (thinly sliced)
- 8 cloves garlic (minced)
- 4 sliced ginger
- 10 dried shiitake mushrooms
- 3 cups hot water
- 1 cup neutral oil
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 2 star anise
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tsp 5 spice
- 1/2 tsp white pepper
- 2 tbsp rock sugar
- 1/4 cup Shaoxing wine
- 1/4 cup light soy sauce
- 3 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 6-8 hard boiled eggs

## Instructions

1. Soak the dried shiitake mushrooms in the hot water until softened; thinly slice the shallots and ginger, and mince the garlic.
2. Heat the neutral oil in a pot and sear the skin-on pork belly until browned on all sides.
3. Add the shallots, garlic, ginger, cinnamon stick, star anise, bay leaves, 5-spice powder, white pepper, and rock sugar; stir until fragrant.
4. Pour in the Shaoxing wine, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and oyster sauce; add the mushrooms with their soaking liquid and the hard-boiled eggs.
5. Bring to a gentle simmer, then braise on low heat until the pork is tender and the sauce has thickened.
6. Serve the pork, eggs, and sauce over steaming rice with cucumber slices on the side.
