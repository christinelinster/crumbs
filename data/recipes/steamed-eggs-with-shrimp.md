## Ingredients

- 6-8 medium or large shrimp, peeled
- 1/2 tablespoon sichuan peppercorns (optional)
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 large eggs
- 1 cup water (2:1 ratio of water to eggs)
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil
- diced spring onion (to garnish)

## Instructions

1. Bring a pot of water to a boil; cook the shrimp (with optional sichuan peppercorns) about 3 minutes until pink, then drain.
2. Beat the eggs with the salt and water, then strain through a fine sieve to remove bubbles.
3. Steam the egg mixture over high heat for about 5 minutes, lay the shrimp on top, and steam on low heat for 1 more minute.
4. Finish with soy sauce, sesame oil, and diced spring onion.
