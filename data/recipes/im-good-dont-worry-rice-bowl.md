## Ingredients

- 200g ground pork or chicken
- 3 eggs
- 1 tbsp rice wine (optional)
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 bowl of rice

### For the sauce

- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 1 tbsp water (slurry)

## Instructions

1. Whisk all the sauce ingredients together in a bowl.
2. Brown the ground meat in hot oil, adding a splash of rice wine if using.
3. Push the meat aside, crack the eggs into the center, and cook them to your preferred doneness.
4. Pour in the sauce and the cornstarch slurry, stirring until the sauce thickens.
5. Spoon everything over a bowl of rice and serve.
