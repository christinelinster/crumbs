## Ingredients

- 6 thinly sliced Persian cucumbers
- 2 cups chopped or shredded chicken breast
- 1/2 red onion, finely diced
- 1 large handful fresh parsley, chopped
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 2-3 tbsp mayo
- zest of 1 lemon
- juice of 1.5 to 2 lemons
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tbsp olive oil
- salt and pepper
- 3-4 tbsp chopped pistachios

## Instructions

1. Slice the cucumbers and combine with the shredded chicken in a large bowl.
2. Dice the onion, soak it in water about 5 minutes, then drain; chop the parsley and add both to the bowl.
3. Whisk the yogurt, mayo, lemon zest and juice, Dijon, olive oil, salt, and pepper into a dressing, seasoning generously.
4. Toss everything with the dressing and finish with the pistachios.
