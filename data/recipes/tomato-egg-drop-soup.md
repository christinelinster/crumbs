## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp oil
- 2-3 tomatoes, cut into small chunks
- 1 cup chicken stock
- 1/2-1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup water (sub chicken stock)
- 1 tsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp white pepper
- 1/2 tsp cornstarch (mixed with 1.5 tbsp water)
- scallion, finely chopped
- salt to taste

## Instructions

1. Soften the tomato chunks in the oil in a soup pot over medium-low heat, about 5 minutes.
2. Add the chicken stock, water, light soy sauce, sesame oil, white pepper, and salt; bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer.
3. Beat the egg in one bowl and mix the cornstarch slurry in another.
4. Stir the soup into a steady swirl with a ladle and slowly pour in the slurry, then stream in the beaten egg at the center of the whirlpool.
5. Serve warm.
